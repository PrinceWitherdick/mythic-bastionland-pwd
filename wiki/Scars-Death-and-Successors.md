# Scars, Death and Successors

Knights get hurt, grow old and die, and the system keeps track of all three: Scars are rolled and kept on the sheet until they settle, a Mortal Wound waits to be patched up, and a Knight who falls puts a card in chat that lets their player carry on at once. This page covers those, the successor every Knight can name, and growing older.

## Scars

A Scar is rolled when Damage leaves a character at exactly 0 GD.

- **From an Attack card**, applying the Damage rolls the Scar for you, on the die that dealt the blow. See [Combat for Players](Combat-for-Players#applying-the-damage).
- **By hand**, click **Roll Scar** under Scars on the Knight sheet. Use this after **Take Damage** from the sheet (which doesn't roll one for you) or whenever the table says one is due.

The **Roll Scar** window asks **The die that dealt the Scar** (preset to the die that dealt it, where known) and has a tick, on by default, to **Record the Scar and apply its Virtue Loss and immediate GD increase**. Untick it to roll and post the card without changing the sheet.

![The Scars section of Sir Oswin Hale's sheet, with a Humiliation Scar, its settle and edit icons showing on hover, and the Roll Scar button](images/scars-death-and-successors-scar.webp)

A Scar card goes to chat with the result, any rolls it called for (where it landed, Virtue lost, GD gained) and what it means. The Scar is added to the Knight's **Scars** list, where it reads like any other row: click its name to show it in chat, hover for the pencil to open it.

### Settling a Scar

Some Scars raise max GD later rather than at once. Until then they wait on the sheet:

- **Hover the Scar's row** to find the hand icon (**Settle this Scar**), drawn faintly even before you hover. Click it when the Scar's moment has come to roll its GD increase.
- Scars that settle with the next Season are settled for you when the Referee turns the Season.
- Scars that settle when tended are settled by **Patch Up** (below).
- A **Doom** Scar is tagged **this Season** while it lasts.

### Humiliation and revenge

A Humiliation dealt by an Attack card remembers who dealt it, and its row is tagged "(revenge on ...)". You can also write or change the name in the Scar's own window, under **Revenge on**.

When that foe is later Mortally Wounded or Slain, a **Revenge?** card goes to chat for the humiliated Knight. If the table agrees it was their revenge, **click Revenge Is Had** to settle the Scar. Only that Knight's player or the Referee can press it. Nothing settles by itself.

## Mortal Wounds and Patch Up

A Knight left Mortally Wounded is down and dying: they can't Attack or use Feats, and the **Mortal Wound** pill is lit.

- **Click Patch Up** under Scars (it shows while someone needs tending) to clear the Mortal Wound and settle any Scar that waits to be tended. A note goes to chat.

Anyone still Mortally Wounded and untended when the Referee ends the Phase dies of it, and the Referee's end-of-Phase card says so.

## When a Knight falls

When a player's Knight is Slain (by Damage, by dying untended, or of old age as an Age turns), the **A Knight Falls** card goes to the whole table. If the **Their ultimate fate was** line on their sheet is empty, it is filled in for them (such as "Slain by" whoever dealt the blow). The fallen Knight's sheet is otherwise left as it was.

![The A Knight Falls card for Dame Isolde Marrow, with Make a New Knight and Take up Hugh Fenwick, Their Successor](images/scars-death-and-successors-fallen.webp)

The card's buttons are the ways their player can carry on. Only that player or the Referee can press them, and only those that apply are shown:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Make a New Knight** | Makes a new blank Knight and opens **Choose a Knight** on them, starting as a Wanderer. If the rest of the Company already has Glory, the chooser offers a **Glory** box to start with some. See [Choosing a Knight](Choosing-a-Knight). |
| **Take up (successor's name), Their Successor** | The Knight's named successor becomes the player's character. |
| **Take up (Squire's name)** | Their Squire is Knighted (each Virtue rises by d6) and the chooser opens to pick which Knight they became. |
| **Take up a Follower** | One of their followers (a companion from their Property, say) carries on. With several, you're asked which. |

When the Referee presses a "take up" button, the fallen Knight's players are given ownership of whoever carries on. A player who doesn't own the successor is asked to let the Referee press **Take up ... Their Successor** for them. Making a new Knight needs Foundry's Create Actors permission.

Each path is written on the Timeline. See [The Campaign Timeline](The-Campaign-Timeline).

## Successors

Every Knight can name a successor in the **Successor** box on the Knight page: another Knight, or a Squire.

- **Click Name a Successor** and pick from every other Knight and Squire in the world. Your own Squire is picked to start with.
- **Or drag another Knight** from the Actors tab onto the sheet.
- **Click the successor's name** to open their sheet, or the × to unname them.

The successor matters in two places: when the Knight falls (the **Take up ... Their Successor** button), and when a new Age begins. As the Referee turns the Age, a Knight who pursues **Succession** names their successor (a Squire named then may be Knighted), and one who pursues **Legacy** passes half their Glory to their successor (they're asked to name one if they haven't). See [Time, Phases and Seasons](Time-Phases-and-Seasons).

## Growing older

- **Click an Age** (Young, Mature, Old) under **Age** on the Knight page to change it.
- Growing older opens **Growing Older**: **Roll Virtues** rolls each Virtue again on d12+d6, keeping the higher on turning Mature and the lower on turning Old, and posts the rolls to chat; **Just change Age** changes the Age and leaves the Virtues alone.
- Going younger (to correct a mistake) just changes the Age.

When the Referee begins a new Age, everyone given an Age who grows a stage older can be ticked to roll the same way.
