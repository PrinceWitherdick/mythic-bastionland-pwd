# Saves, Luck and Feats

Most rolls a player makes outside an Attack are a Save against one of their Virtues or a Luck Roll, and both start from the Knight sheet. This page covers those, the three Feats, getting Virtues and Guard back, and the condition pills that track what has happened to your Knight.

## Rolling a Save

- **Click a Virtue's name** (Vigour, Clarity or Spirit) on the Knight sheet. A d20 is rolled against the Virtue's current score, and a card goes to chat saying what was needed ("Needs 12 or less"), the roll, and **Pass** or **Fail**.

![A Vigour Save card in chat for Dame Isolde Marrow, with the d20 drawn on a die shape, Needs 1 or less under the title, and Fail](images/saves-luck-and-feats-save-card.webp)

The Save uses the current score, not the maximum, so a Knight worn down by Damage or Virtue Loss saves at the lower number.

Saves are also made for you elsewhere: by the Attack dialog for Smite, by the Attack card for Focus and Deny, and by the Referee's tools for Morale, Reaction and Surprise.

> If your Knight has an Ability that etches a rune for a number, and a Save's d20 lands on that number, you're asked whether to turn the die to another face before the result counts.

## The Luck Roll

When something comes down to chance, anyone can make the Luck Roll: a single d6, high in the players' favour and low against them.

- **Click Luck Roll** on the Knight sheet's action bar, or **the Luck Roll macro in hotbar slot 1** (every player gets it there).

A window asks how to read the die:

- **The Luck Roll table** (the default) tells how things turn out, with its three results listed beside the rolls that give them. Use it when nobody has named the odds.
- **Stated odds** only answer yes or no, for when the Referee has already said how likely it is: **A high chance** (2+), **Likely** (3+), **Straight 50/50** (4+), **Unlikely** (5+) or **A slim chance** (6). The card says whether fortune favours the players.

## Feats

**Feats** on the Knight sheet lists Smite, Focus and Deny, each with the Virtue Save it costs ("VIG Save or become Fatigued").

- **Clicking a Feat on the sheet only shows it to the table.** Its tooltip says where it is really performed.
- **Smite** is ticked in the Attack window, which makes its Save.
- **Focus** and **Deny** are pressed on an Attack card, which makes their Save.

A failed Save leaves the Knight **Fatigued**. A Fatigued, Slain or Mortally Wounded Knight can't perform a Feat at all, and is told so. See [Combat for Players](Combat-for-Players#feats-in-a-fight).

## Recovery and Rest

The **Recovery** box on the Knight page lists what brings each Virtue and Guard back, with a button beside each:

- **Click the heart** beside VIG, CLA or SPI to **Restore** that Virtue to its maximum. A note goes to chat.
- **Click the mug** beside GD/Fatigue to **Rest**: GD back to full, no longer Fatigued, and no longer Exposed (unless CLA 0 is what Exposes them). A note goes to chat.
- **Indulge your Passion** with the wine glass on its row to restore SPI.
- **Use a Remedy** with the flask on its row: tick everyone present, and each has that Virtue restored. Using a Remedy takes a whole Phase, so the Referee moves the calendar on (a player's click asks the Referee to).

When a Combat ends, the Referee is offered a rest for everyone in it who needs one, in one go.

## Conditions

The **Conditions** box shows a pill for each condition, lit when it applies. Hover any pill to read what it does.

| Pill | How it's set |
| --- | --- |
| **Fatigued** | Click to switch. Set by a failed Feat Save; cleared by Rest. |
| **Exposed** | Click to switch. Also shown whenever CLA is 0. Cleared by Rest. |
| **Wounded** | Click to switch. Set when Damage gets past GD; lapses once VIG is whole again. |
| **Mortal Wound** | Click to switch. Set by Damage; **Patch Up** under Scars clears it. |
| **Slain** | Click to switch. Set when Damage takes VIG to 0. Raising VIG above 0 lifts it. |
| **Mounted** | Click to switch. The Attack dialog also sets it from its own tick. |
| **Exhausted** | Lights by itself at VIG 0 from Virtue Loss (not Slain). |
| **Impaired** | Lights by itself at SPI 0. |

The same box also shows, each with an × to clear it:

- **Afflictions** (a sickness, a burning acid) with what they take and how often. **Click the name** to take its toll now, when the Referee says it's due; **click the ×** to cure it. Daily afflictions take their toll by themselves each morning.
- **Dice held for the fight**, such as an Ability's die. They lapse when the fight ends; the × drops one early.
- **Marks left by a Gambit** that landed on your Knight. In a Combat they lapse by themselves; the × clears one once it has run its course. See [Combat for Players](Combat-for-Players#gambit-marks).

Armour that counts only when Mounted or Wounded follows these pills, so keep them true.

## Morale

Morale doesn't touch player characters, but you'll see its card when the Referee rolls it for NPCs: a SPI Save, and on a failure **Fled** and **Surrendered** buttons that mark the NPCs and take them out of the Combat's turn order. Only the Referee or the NPC's owner can press them. See [Running Combat](Running-Combat).

## Rule words on cards and sheets

Rule words such as Exposed, Hefty or Gambit are printed in bold in the system's windows and chat cards. **Hover one** to see what it means. Turn this off with **Rule Word Popups** on your Settings page. See [Your Settings and Accessibility](Your-Settings-and-Accessibility).
