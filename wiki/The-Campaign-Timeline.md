# The Campaign Timeline

The Timeline is the campaign's record, Season by Season. It has a thread for the Company, one for each Knight (Squires too), one for each Domain and one for each Realm. Most of it writes itself as you play: Seasons turning, Myths completed, Ranks reached, Scars, deaths, Domains founded, places first reached. Anyone at the table can read it, and anyone can add their own entries.

![The Timeline window laid Down, with a column each for the Company, the three Knights, the Squire, the Domain and the Realm, five Seasons of cards in rows, the Season it is now marked Now, and the Filter, Colours and Down/Across buttons in t](images/the-campaign-timeline-board.webp)

## Opening it

| Where | What it shows |
|---|---|
| The **Timeline** window | Every thread side by side. Players have it on their hotbar (see below). **Open the Timeline** on any sheet's Timeline tab opens it too. |
| **Timeline** tab on a Knight's or Squire's sheet | That character's own thread. |
| **Timeline** tab on a Domain's sheet | The Domain's own thread. |
| **Timeline** page on the [Referee Toolkit](The-Referee-Toolkit) rail | The full board, as the window shows it. **Open in a Window** pops it out. |
| The **Timeline** journal entry | One page per thread, to read in the Journal sidebar. It can't be written on there; it says "This thread is written from the Timeline window." |

**Players' hotbar:** each player gets a **Timeline** macro, once, usually in slot 3 after the Luck Roll and Places. A Referee gets one only in [Solo Play](Solo-Play); otherwise the Toolkit's Timeline page does the job. See [Macros and Hotkeys](Macros-and-Hotkeys).

The Timeline journal is made the first time a Referee joins the world. Until then the window says "The Timeline is made the next time a Referee joins the game."

## Threads

- **Company**: the story of the whole party. Season turns, sessions, Myths, Omens, arrivals, who joined, who fell.
- **Knight**: one per Knight. Played Knights get a thread at once; a Knight nobody plays gets one the first time something is written about them. A Squire's thread carries on after they are Knighted.
- **Domain**: one per Domain, with its Crises, Court, Council, rulers and Warbands.
- **Realm**: one per Realm map, with Myths, Omens, places found and Holdings granted there.

A Knight or Domain that no player can see (for example a rival's) has its thread kept for the Referee alone. Rows about anyone kept from the players stay off the threads the players read.

## Reading it

- **Seasons.** Each row (or column) is a Season that has something in it, oldest first, with **Before the Tale** ahead of them all. A new Age starts with a heading such as **The Second Age**. The year is never shown: the same Season again reads "Harvest, a Year On". The Season it is now wears a **Now** badge and, where you can write, is drawn even when nothing is in it yet.
- **Season turns** sit in the Season's heading, not as cards. On the full board the heading carries the Company's turn. On a Knight's or Domain's tab the heading carries the Company's turns too, but only across the Seasons that thread covers.
- **Cards** show a title, a chip on the right naming its kind (or its tag), a place with a pin, and what happened. Links in a card open what they name.
- **Down and Across.** The two arrow buttons at the end of the toolbar lay the Timeline down the page (a column per thread) or across it (a row per thread). This choice is kept in your browser.
- **Filter.** Untick kinds of entry under **Show** (Written by Hand, Seasons and Sessions, Myths, Glory, Scars and Death, Squires and Successors, Domains and Holdings, Travels, then the table's own tags). On the full board, **Threads** lets you untick whole threads, grouped as Company, Knights, Domains and Realms. The button shows a badge such as **2 hidden** while anything is off; **Show All** ticks everything again. Your Filter is kept in your browser and hides, never deletes.
- **The scrubber** at the foot appears once the Timeline spans two Ages or more. Slide it to bring an Age into view, and it follows as you scroll. On a single thread with fewer than two Ages it stops at each Season instead.

A player who owns a Knight or Domain opens the full board showing only the Company's thread and their own, until they change the Filter. The Referee sees every thread.

## Moving about

The board scrolls like any page, and has a few extras:

- **Drag an empty spot** with the left button to pan. **Right-drag** pans from anywhere, even over a card.
- **Let go while moving** and the board slides on a little (not with reduced motion on).
- **Ctrl+wheel** (Cmd+wheel on a Mac) zooms about the pointer, from a quarter size to four times. The wheel alone scrolls.
- **On the scrubber**, the arrow keys and Page Up/Down step one Age (or Season); Home and End jump to either end.
- A Timeline opens on the Season it is now. One that was open when you reloaded comes back where you left it, zoom and all.

## Writing an entry

- **Click New Entry** in the toolbar. In the window you are asked **Write on Which Thread?** first; on a sheet's tab it goes on that thread.
- **Hover a Season and click + Add** to write an entry already dated to that Season, on the thread whose column (or row) you are in. The **+ Add** in the Season it is now shows while that Season is empty.

The entry window has:

- **When**: **Now**, any Season already on the Timeline (newest first), or **Before the Tale**.
- **Tag**: **None**, one of the kinds the game records (the entry then files under that kind in the Filter), or one of the table's own tags.
- **Title**, **Place** and **What Happened**.

Hidden tricks in the entry window:

- **Drop a Scene, an actor or a Journal entry on Place** to make the place a link. The field shows **Linked**; type in it again to make it a plain name. Place also suggests names already on the Timeline and Realms and Domains you can see.
- **Drop a Scene, an actor, an Item or a Journal entry on What Happened** to write a link to it where the caret is.
- **New Tag** makes a tag for the whole table, with a name and a colour (for example a "Rumours" tag for hearsay). Press **Enter** in the tag's name to add it; Enter there adds the tag rather than saving the entry. A name already in use gives you the existing tag.

## Changing and removing entries

**Hover a card** and a row of buttons appears at its foot:

- **Move earlier** and **Move later** reorder it within its Season. To move it to another Season, edit it and change **When**; it goes to the end of that Season.
- **Edit this entry** reopens the entry window. Entries the game wrote have no Tag field, but their title, place, wording and Season can be changed, and your changes stick.
- **Remove this entry** asks first.

A Season's turn in a heading has its own **Edit** and **Remove** buttons on hover.

## What the game writes for you

Each entry lands in the Season it happened, on every thread it concerns. The chip on the card says what wrote it.

| Filter line | What writes it | Threads |
|---|---|---|
| Seasons and Sessions | A Season or an Age turns (**Season's End**, **Age's End**, **Distant Realm**), with what befell each Knight and Domain. | Company, each Knight something befell, each Domain |
| | A session is ended from the [Ending a Session](Ending-a-Session) window (**Session**): plans and time passed always; the Referee's recap only with **Write it on the Timeline too, for everyone to read** ticked. | Company |
| | Somebody grows into an older Age (**Aging**). | That Knight |
| Myths | A Myth is completed (**Myth**). Unresolving it takes the rows off again. | Company, Realm, Knights given Glory for it |
| | An Omen is met, or heard of at Court (**Omen**). The row names only the Myth's number and where the Company stood. Taking an Omen back removes it. | Company, Realm |
| Glory | Glory raised by the system reaches a new Rank (**Rank**). Glory typed into the sheet by hand writes nothing. | That Knight |
| | A duel ends, with who won and the Glory it moved (**Duel**). Sparring writes nothing. | Both duelists |
| | Glory for a tournament or a battle (**Glory**). | Each Knight, and the Company |
| Scars and Death | A Scar is taken, settled, or avenged (**Scar**, **Scar Settled**, **Revenge**). | That Knight |
| | A Mortal Wound, or a Knight taking one in someone's place (**Mortal Wound**, **Ward**). | Those involved |
| | A Knight falls (**Death**). | That Knight, Company |
| Squires and Successors | A Knight joins the Company (**Joined**), takes a Squire (**Squire**), names a successor (**Successor**); a Squire is Knighted (**Knighted**); someone takes up a fallen Knight's journey (**Succeeded**, **Took Up**). | Those involved; joins also go on the Company's |
| Domains and Holdings | A Domain is founded; its Crisis Roll, a Crisis resolved, Drama, a Council task settled, a Grand Design begun; a new ruler by succession or conquest; a Holding granted; Court and Circle joins and leaves; Warbands mustered and dismissed. | The Domain, plus the Knight or Realm concerned |
| Travels | The Company first reaches a hex with a Holding, a Landmark or a Myth, as the players know it (**Arrival**), or reveals a Landmark (**Found**). | Company, Realm |
| | The players' own note on a hex (**Party Note**). Rewriting it updates the row; clearing it removes the row. | Company, Realm |

Saves, Luck, weather, Spark Table and People rolls, and the Chronicle are not written on the Timeline. See [Time, Phases and Seasons](Time-Phases-and-Seasons), [Myths and Omens](Myths-and-Omens) and [Domains and Courts](Domains-and-Courts) for the events themselves.

## What players can and can't do

- **Can** read every thread except those kept for the Referee.
- **Can** write, edit, reorder and remove entries on any thread they can read, including entries the game wrote. The Timeline is shared, as in Stonetop, so a player can keep their Knight's thread with no Referee online.
- **Can** make new tags for the whole table.
- **Can't** see or write on a thread kept for the Referee, or read rows about anyone kept from them.
- **Can't** change the colours, or turn the Timeline on or off.

## Colours (Referee)

**Colours** in the toolbar sets the colour each kind of entry wears on its card edge and chip, for everyone. A colour shows on the Timeline as you pick it; nobody else sees it until you click **Save**. The arrow beside a kind puts it back to its own colour. Closing the menu any way but **Save** drops the changes.

## Turning it off

The world setting **Campaign Timeline** (in Configure Settings, or the Settings page of the Referee Toolkit) turns the whole feature off: after a reload the sheet tabs and the Toolkit page go, no new hotbar macros are handed out, and opening the window only says "The Timeline is turned off for this world." The game keeps writing in the background, so nothing is lost when you turn it back on. See [World Settings Reference](World-Settings-Reference).

> **Did you know?**
> - When a Knight is renamed, their thread is renamed too. A fallen Knight's thread keeps their name after the actor is deleted.
> - If you share or hide a thread's journal page by hand, it stays that way until that Knight's or Domain's own permissions change.
> - Macro writers can open the window with `game.system.api.openTimeline()`.
