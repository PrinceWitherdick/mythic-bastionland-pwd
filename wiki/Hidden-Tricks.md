# Hidden Tricks

A cheat sheet of the things you would never find by looking: keys, things you can drag, right-clicks, double-clicks and hovers. Each links to the page that explains it properly.

## Keys

| Key | Who | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| `B` | Referee, and players when allowed | Opens the rulebook, or closes it when it is in front. [More](The-Rulebook-Reader) |
| `6` | Referee | Opens or closes the Spark Tables, ahead of the hotbar's own `6`. [More](Macros-and-Hotkeys) |
| `C` | Referee | Opens the Referee Toolkit, which is each Referee's character. [More](The-Referee-Toolkit) |
| `Ctrl`+`Z`, `Ctrl`+`Y` | Referee | Undo and redo Realm edits, while the Realm tools are in use. `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`Z` also redoes. [More](Painting-and-Editing-the-Realm) |

## The Knight sheet

- **Drag an NPC** from the Actors tab onto a Knight's sheet to make it their steed. [More](Property-Steeds-and-Squires)
- **Drag a Squire** onto a Knight's sheet to make them that Knight's Squire. [More](Property-Steeds-and-Squires)
- **Drag another Knight** onto a Knight's sheet to name them the successor. [More](Scars-Death-and-Successors)
- **Drag a Domain** from the Actors tab onto a Knight's sheet to make it the Domain they rule. [More](Domains-and-Courts)
- **Click a possession's name** to post it to chat. [More](Property-Steeds-and-Squires)
- **Hover a possession's row** to bring up its pencil, which opens the item. The pencil stays hidden otherwise. [More](Property-Steeds-and-Squires)
- **Hover a portrait** on a sheet, or a picture in the Knight and Myth choosers, to see it larger beside it. Turn this off with **Larger Art on Hover**. [More](Your-Settings-and-Accessibility)
- **Click the portrait**, then **Frame** in the portrait window's title bar, to choose the part of the picture the sheet shows. [More](The-Knight-Sheet)
- In the framer, **drag inside the box** to move it, **drag a corner** to resize it, or **drag outside it** to draw a new one. **Arrow keys** nudge it (`Shift` for bigger steps), and **`+` / `-`** grow or shrink it. Nothing is kept until **Save**. [More](The-Knight-Sheet)
- **Right-click a Knight's Token** for a HUD button that hides or shows the heraldry badge on it. [More](Heraldry)

## Chat cards

- **`Shift`-click Apply Damage** on an Attack card to open the **Take Damage** window for each target, instead of taking the Damage as the card has it. [More](Running-Combat)
- **Hover a hex chip** on a card, such as a Wilderness Roll's, to ring that hex in green on the map. [More](Exploring-the-Wilderness)
- **Click a hex chip** to take your own map to that Realm and hex. Nobody else's view moves. [More](Exploring-the-Wilderness)
- **Click a page number** such as "(p16)" or "page 151" to open the rulebook there. [More](The-Rulebook-Reader#page-links)
- **Hover a bold rule word** such as Exposed, Hefty or VIG to see what it means. [More](The-Rulebook-Reader#rule-word-popups)

## The Realm map

- **Double-click open ground** on a Realm, with the Token tools in hand, to open that hex in Places. Players can open only hexes they know. [More](Travels-and-Places)
- **Double-click the Company's Token** to open the hex it stands in, in Places. [More](Travels-and-Places)
- **Right-click the Company's Token** (Referee) for a HUD button that changes the Company's picture. [More](Travels-and-Places)
- **Right-click the footprints button** beside the sidebar to choose how visited hexes are marked, and in what colour. [More](Travels-and-Places)
- **Right-click while asked to click a hex**, or while carrying the Company to a hex, to give up; `Esc` does the same. A right-drag still pans the map. [More](Making-a-Realm)

**Painting the Realm** (Referee, with the Realm's paint tool) [More](Painting-and-Editing-the-Realm)

- **Right-click** takes away what the brush lays: a Barrier along an edge, the Holding or Landmark in a hex, or a river through it.
- **`Shift`-click** empties a hex of its Holding or Landmark, or trims a river there. On a Barrier's edge it switches the Barrier between hidden and revealed.
- **`Alt`-click** picks up the terrain, Holding or Landmark under the pointer as your brush.
- **Click a hex that already has the brush's terrain** to step it on to that terrain's next picture.

**Lining up an imported map** (Referee) [More](Making-a-Realm)

- **Drag** anywhere on the map to slide the picture under the hexes.
- **Arrow keys** nudge it one pixel; with `Shift`, ten.
- **`Ctrl` + mouse wheel** sizes the picture evenly about the pointer; add `Shift` for bigger steps.
- **`Enter`** keeps it; **`Esc`** puts it back.

## Places and the chart

- **`Ctrl` + mouse wheel** over the **Chart** zooms it about the pointer. [More](Travels-and-Places)
- **Double-click the chart** to zoom in there; double-click again, or press **Whole chart**, to see all of it. [More](Travels-and-Places)
- **Drag the chart with either mouse button** once it is zoomed in, and flick it to let it slide on. [More](Travels-and-Places)

## The Referee Toolkit and the time bar

- **Drag an actor onto a Myth's card** in the Toolkit to count them among that Myth's Cast, or onto the City Quest for the City's. [More](Myths-and-Omens)
- **Click the time bar** at the top of the screen (Referee) for **Weeks Pass**, **Months Pass**, **A Year Passes** and **End the Session**. A click elsewhere or `Esc` puts them away. [More](Time-Phases-and-Seasons)

## The Campaign Timeline

- **`Ctrl` + mouse wheel** zooms the Timeline about the pointer. [More](The-Campaign-Timeline)
- **Drag the board with either mouse button**, anywhere but on a button or link, to pan it; flick it to let it slide on. [More](The-Campaign-Timeline)
- In an entry, **drop a Scene, an actor or a Journal entry on Place** to link it. Typing there again makes it a plain name. [More](The-Campaign-Timeline)
- **Drop a Scene, actor, Item or Journal entry on What Happened** to write a link to it where the cursor is. [More](The-Campaign-Timeline)
- **Press `Enter` in a new tag's name** to add the tag, rather than saving the entry. [More](The-Campaign-Timeline)

## The heraldry painter

Open it by clicking the shield on a Knight's sheet. [More](Heraldry)

- **`Alt`-click the shield** to take a colour from it, whatever tool is in hand.
- **Hold `Shift`** while drawing with the **Line** tool to keep the line to the nearest 45 degrees.
- **Arrow keys** move the charge or picture you picked (`Shift` for bigger steps). **`+` / `-`**, or the **mouse wheel** over the shield, resize it.
- **`Delete`** or **`Backspace`** removes the charge or picture you picked.
- **`Esc`** or **`Enter`** lets go of it. `Esc` also puts the charge gallery away.
- **`Ctrl`+`Z`** undoes, and **`Ctrl`+`Y`** or **`Ctrl`+`Shift`+`Z`** redoes (`Cmd` on a Mac).

## Drop-down lists

Every drop-down in the system's windows opens a list of its own. [More](Your-Settings-and-Accessibility)

- **Type the first letters** of an option to jump to it.
- **`Alt` + an arrow key**, an arrow key, `Space` or `F4` opens a list from the keyboard.
- **`Home`**, **`End`**, **`Page Up`** and **`Page Down`** move through a long list. **`Enter`** or **`Tab`** picks; **`Esc`** closes without picking.

## The rulebook

- **`Ctrl` + mouse wheel** in the reader zooms the book about the pointer, never the browser. [More](The-Rulebook-Reader#reading)
- **Drag the page** to move about it: the reader opens with the hand tool. [More](The-Rulebook-Reader#reading)
- **The eye in the reader's title bar** (Referee) opens the same page on every player's screen. [More](The-Rulebook-Reader#showing-a-page-to-the-table)
