# Heraldry

Every Knight and Squire has a shield in the top right of their sheet, and you paint its arms yourself in the heraldry painter: paint by hand, divide the field, place charges from a gallery of over two hundred public-domain drawings, roll random arms, or drop in a picture of your own. The finished shield shows on the sheet and as a small badge on the Knight's Token, and flies over any Holding their Domain rules on the Realm map.

## Opening the painter

- **Click the shield** on the Knight sheet (its tooltip reads **Paint heraldry**). Only someone who can edit the Knight can open it; everyone else just sees the shield.

![The Heraldry of Dame Isolde Marrow window, with her shield of a golden sun on blue, the Brush, Line, Fill, Eraser and Eyedropper tools, the tinctures, Divide the shield, Add a charge, Randomize and the Layers list](images/heraldry-painter.webp)

The window has three parts:

- **Along the top**: the tools (**Brush**, **Line**, **Fill**, **Eraser**, **Eyedropper**), then Undo, Redo and **Clear the shield**.
- **In the middle**: the shield, with **Add a charge**, **Randomize** and the **Layers** list under it.
- **Down the right**: the **Tinctures**, **Other colour**, **Recent colours**, **Divide the shield** and **Brush size**. This side changes to the charge gallery, the arms controls or the picture controls when you are working on one of those.
- **Along the bottom**: **Browse files**, **Download** and **Save**.

## Painting by hand

- **Pick a tincture** (Or, Argent, Gules, Azure, Vert, Purpure, Sable, Tenné, Sanguine, Murrey; hover one for its plain colour name), or any colour from **Other colour**. Colours you pick there are kept under **Recent colours**.
- **Brush** paints freehand, **Eraser** rubs out, and **Brush size** sets how wide both are.
- **Line** draws a straight line: drag from one end to the other. Hold **Shift** to snap it level, upright or diagonal.
- **Fill** pours the colour in hand into the area you click.
- **Eyedropper** takes a colour from the shield. **Alt-click** does the same with any tool in hand.
- **Divide the shield**: pick a division (Per Pale, Per Bend, Quarterly, Gyronny and so on). Guide lines appear on the shield, and clicking a part paints it, and the parts matching it, in the colour in hand.

## Charges

- **Click Add a charge** to open the gallery. Pick a group from the **Show** menu (Beasts, Birds, Monsters, Arms and armour, Castles and crowns, Sun, moon and stars, Plants, Crosses and symbols) and click a charge to place it in the colour in hand. Hover a charge to see its name and the book it was drawn after.
- **Click Back** or press `Esc` to put the gallery away.

A placed charge is live until you're done with it:

- **Drag it** to move it.
- **Scroll the mouse wheel** over the shield, or use the **Size** slider, to resize it about the pointer.
- **Fit**, **Fill** and **Flip** size it to the shield or mirror it.
- **Recolour it** with the swatches.
- **Remove** takes it off; **Done** goes back to the paints.

## Random arms

**Randomize** rolls a whole coat of arms: a plain or divided field and perhaps a charge, in tinctures that read well against each other. The field and charge go on layers of their own, and anything else on the shield stays.

The side then shows the **Arms** controls, where you can change any part by hand or roll just that part again with its own die:

- **Field**: pick another division or **Plain**, or roll another field.
- **Tinctures**: change each part's colour, or roll new tinctures for the field and charge.
- **Charge**: roll another charge (keeping its colours and place), pick another from the gallery, take it off, resize it, **Fit** or **Flip** it, or **Counterchange** it so it takes the field's colours swapped about part by part. Drag the charge to move it, and scroll over it to resize it.
- **Paint by hand** goes back to the paints. **Edit arms** under the shield brings these controls back later.

## Layers

Each thing you paint, place or roll goes on a layer of its own, listed under the shield with the front first.

- **Click a layer** to pick it: a charge or picture becomes movable again, and the arms bring back their controls.
- **Bring forward** and **Send back** reorder it; **Remove layer** deletes it.
- **New** adds an empty layer to paint on, straight above the one picked.

Painting with no layer picked goes on the front layer if it's a paint layer, or a new one if not. The layers are saved with the Knight, so next time you open the painter everything is still separate and editable.

## Your own picture

- **Drag an image file** from your computer onto the shield, or **click Browse files** (if you're allowed to browse files), to place a picture on a layer of its own. Move and size it like a charge.
- **Download** saves the design to your computer as a full-size PNG. Opening that same file again (by dropping it or browsing to it) brings it back as the whole painting, in place of every layer.

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Ctrl`+`Z` | Undo. Each notch of the wheel or run of nudges counts as one step. |
| `Ctrl`+`Y` or `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`Z` | Redo. (Use `Cmd` on a Mac.) |
| `Alt`-click | Take a colour from the shield, whatever tool is in hand. |
| `Shift` while drawing a line | Snap it level, upright or diagonal. |
| Arrow keys | Nudge the picked charge or picture. Hold `Shift` for bigger steps. |
| `+` / `-` | Make the picked charge or picture bigger or smaller. |
| `Delete` or `Backspace` | Remove the picked charge or picture. |
| `Esc` or `Enter` | Let go of the picked charge or picture. `Esc` also closes the gallery. |

The keys on the shield need it to have the focus: click it first. While the painter has the focus, `Esc` and `Ctrl`+`Z` stay inside it, so they never close the window or undo something on the map behind it.

## Saving

**Save** stores the painted shield on the Knight and closes the window. If you're not allowed to upload files, the shield is kept on the Knight itself; a very detailed design may then be too large to keep its separate layers (it is saved as one picture, and a warning says so) or too large to keep at all. Ask the Referee for upload permission if that happens.

## On the Token

A Knight with painted heraldry shows their shield as a small badge at the top right of their Token.

- **Right-click the Token** and click the shield button in the left column of the Token HUD to **Hide heraldry** on that one Token, or **Show heraldry** again. Only the Token's owners see the button.

A Knight's heraldry can also be borne by their Domain. See [Domains and Courts](Domains-and-Courts).
