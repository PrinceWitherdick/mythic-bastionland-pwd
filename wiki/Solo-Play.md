# Solo Play

For playing Mythic Bastionland alone. You are the Referee and the Company both, so the Realm keeps its secrets from you as it would from players: its **Myths**, **Landmarks** and **Barriers** stay hidden until the Company finds them by travelling. The terrain, the rivers and the Holdings stay open, and there is no fog over the map.

## Turning it on

- Tick **Solo Mode** in the Welcome window of a new world.
- Or turn on **Solo Play** in Foundry's Configure Settings, or on the Referee Toolkit's Settings page under **For the Referee**.

It is a world setting. Turning it on or off redraws the map and every open window at once.

## How the secrets are found

- **Myths.** In the Referee Toolkit, a Myth is listed as **A Myth not yet met** until the Company meets one of its Omens; then it is named. Its hex is shown on the map once the Company stands in it, with a notice that the Company has found it.
- **Landmarks** are found the way the book has it: by the Wilderness Roll. A Landmark the roll finds is revealed on the map. Simply walking into its hex doesn't find it.
- **Barriers.** A hidden Barrier your own Company Token runs into is revealed, as it would be for a player.
- **Folklore and vantage points.** The cards from Ask Somebody and Look from a Vantage Point mark what they name on the map by themselves, rather than offering a button to do it.
- **A new Myth** that comes into the Realm, when one is resolved, is announced only by its number: it will be known by its first Omen.
- **A Vantage point's** list of what stands in the hexes around says only that something is there.

## What changes for you

Everything a Referee would normally see that would give a secret away keeps it instead:

- **The map** doesn't draw hidden Myths, Landmarks or Barriers for you.
- **The hex readout** under the map names only what has been found.
- **No Realm Key** is whispered when a Realm is made, and **Keep this Realm?** has no **Choose the Myths**: which Myths the Realm holds is left to the dice, unseen.
- **The Referee Toolkit**'s Myths and Places pages show only what has been found.
- **Edit this hex** won't change what stands in a hex, so nothing still to be found is given away. Terrain, Barriers and the rest still can be changed.
- Places' **Lay of the Land** shows only what has been found. A Myth's tab shows the current Omen, not the next, and has no **Revealed** toggle; you can still step the Omens seen.
- **The Wilderness card** doesn't say which Myths a roll chose between.

Places opens with the players' view as its own, since in solo play you are the players.

## The Solo Play tag

![The Players list in the lower left with the small Solo Play tag above the names, and the Solo Play window it opens beside it, with Turn Off, Let Friends Join and Keep Playing Alone](images/solo-play-tag.webp)

While solo play is on, a small **Solo Play** tag sits over the Players list, for Referees only. Hover it for a reminder; **click it** for a window that explains what solo play keeps from you, with two buttons:

- **Keep Playing Alone**, the default, closes it.
- **Turn Off, Let Friends Join** turns solo play off. You then see the whole Realm at once, every secret still to be found included, while the players still see only what they have found.

Solo play can be turned back on from the Toolkit's Settings page.

## Your hotbar

A Referee in a solo world also gets the players' **Places** and **Timeline** macros on their hotbar, once, since you are the Company too. See [Macros and Hotkeys](Macros-and-Hotkeys).
