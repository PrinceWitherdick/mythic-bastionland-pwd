# Solo Play

For playing Mythic Bastionland alone. You are the Referee and the Company both, so the Realm keeps its secrets from you as it would from players: its **Myths**, **Landmarks** and **Barriers** stay hidden until the Company finds them by travelling. The terrain, the rivers and the Holdings stay open, and there is no fog over the map.

## Turning it on

- Tick **Solo Play** in the Welcome window of a new world.
- Or turn on **Solo Play** in Foundry's Configure Settings, or on the Referee Toolkit's Settings page under **For the Referee**.

It is a world setting. Turning it on or off redraws the map and every open window at once. Turned on partway through a campaign, the Myths in hexes the Company has already been to are found straight away, as if it had been on all along.

## Your Knights

You own every Knight in a solo world, so the system can't tell your Company from the Knights you run as Referee, such as a Holding's ruler, by who owns them. Each Knight's sheet has a **Rides with the Company** tick in its title bar for this. It starts ticked. Untick it on your NPC Knights.

A Knight who rides with the Company:

- moves as the Company on the map when there is no Company Token;
- is ticked in the Season's pursuits and the morning's checks;
- goes first in a fight and doesn't make Morale Saves;
- gets a Timeline thread, and is offered the ways on if Slain;
- counts towards the Company's size for Squires, and for the City Quest once a Knight-Radiant.

## Drawing your own Realm

When you choose **Draw it myself** or import a map in a solo world, the New Realm window has a **Secrets** page. It asks who places the Realm's Myths, Landmarks and Barriers:

- **The dice place them, unseen.** You draw the terrain, rivers and Holdings. When you press **Finish the Realm**, the Myths, Landmarks and Barriers are rolled by the book's rules around what you drew, and hidden from you until the Company finds them. Creating a Realm shows those steps without their tools, and Finish doesn't count them as missing.
- **I place them, then they're hidden.** You place them yourself and see them as you draw. When you finish, they're hidden from you, as if someone else had placed them.
- **I place them, and keep them in view.** You place them yourself and they stay shown: every Myth named and its hex known, every Landmark and Barrier found.

This is settled the first time you finish. If you take the Realm back into drawing later, what's still to be found stays hidden.

## How the secrets are found

- **Myths.** In the Referee Toolkit, a Myth is listed as **A Myth not yet met** until the Company meets one of its Omens; then it is named. Its hex is shown on the map once the Company stands in it, with a notice that the Company has found it. That's the hex the Company really ends up in: a blind drift lands it elsewhere, and dire weather keeps it where it was. A Company Token first set down in a Myth's hex finds it too, and so does a sweep of the hex with **Search**.
- **Landmarks** are found the way the book has it: by the Wilderness Roll. A Landmark the roll finds is revealed on the map. Simply walking into its hex doesn't find it.
- **Barriers.** A hidden Barrier your own Company Token runs into is revealed, as it would be for a player.
- **Folklore and vantage points.** The cards from Ask Somebody and Look from a Vantage Point mark what they name on the map by themselves, rather than offering a button to do it.
- **Rumours.** Somebody who knows only roughly where a Myth lies, a Knight or Vagabond asked for Folklore or a person rolled up in a Holding, speaks of it by its number and its way: "Myth 3, three Hexes to the north-east". Its name comes with its first Omen, with its hex, or from a Seer, who knows where every Myth lies.
- **A new Myth** that comes into the Realm, when one is resolved, is announced only by its number: it will be known by its first Omen.
- **A Vantage point's** list of what stands in the hexes around says only that something is there.

## What changes for you

Everything a Referee would normally see that would give a secret away keeps it instead:

- **The map** doesn't draw hidden Myths, Landmarks or Barriers for you.
- **The hex readout** under the map names only what has been found.
- **No Realm Key** is whispered when a Realm is made, and **Keep this Realm?** has no **Choose the Myths**: which Myths the Realm holds is left to the dice, unseen.
- **The Referee Toolkit**'s Myths and Places pages show only what has been found.
- **Edit this hex** won't change what stands in a hex, so nothing still to be found is given away. Terrain and the rest still can be changed there, and Barriers with the map's brush.
- **The paint palette** counts only what has been found, and its brushes leave alone a hex or edge that holds something still to be found.
- **The Seer chooser** lists only the Sanctums you have found.
- **Foundry's Tiles and Drawings lists** in the sidebar leave out what's still hidden.
- **A Realm Key** whispered before you turned solo play on is set aside in the chat log.
- Places' **Lay of the Land** shows only what has been found. A Myth's tab shows the current Omen, not the next, and has no **Revealed** toggle; you can still step the Omens seen. Its Wilderness Roll, Ask Somebody, Search and Vantage buttons show only for the hex the Company stands in.
- **The Wilderness card** doesn't say which Myths a roll chose between, nor show the die that chose.

Places opens with the players' view as its own, since in solo play you are the players.

## The Solo Play tag

![The Players list in the lower left with the small Solo Play tag above the names, and the Solo Play window it opens beside it, with Turn Off, Let Friends Join and Keep Playing Alone](images/solo-play-tag.webp)

While solo play is on, a small **Solo Play** tag sits over the Players list, for Referees only. Hover it for a reminder; **click it** for a window that explains what solo play keeps from you, with two buttons:

- **Keep Playing Alone**, the default, closes it.
- **Turn Off, Let Friends Join** turns solo play off. You then see the whole Realm at once, every secret still to be found included, while the players still see only what they have found.

Solo play can be turned back on from the Toolkit's Settings page.

## Your hotbar

A Referee in a solo world also gets the players' **Places** and **Timeline** macros on their hotbar, once, since you are the Company too. They arrive as soon as solo play is turned on. See [Macros and Hotkeys](Macros-and-Hotkeys).
