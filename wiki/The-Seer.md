# The Seer

Every Knight in the book was knighted by a Seer, printed on the same page. The Knight sheet keeps that Seer on a page of its own: their name, picture and what the book says of them, filled in from your imported rulebook, with a box for your own notes. The Referee can also bring any Seer into the world as an NPC, and pick the Seer for every Sanctum on the Realm map.

## The Seer page

**Click the eye** on the Knight sheet's tab rail to open the **Seer** page.

![The Seer page of a Knight sheet, "Knighted by" with the Seer's name at the top, the Seer's portrait on the left, their stat line and description from the book on the right, and the Notes box below](images/the-seer-page.webp)

- **Knighted by** is the Seer's name. Type it in, or leave it: a Knight chosen from the book has their Seer named and filled in already.
- **The picture** beside it is the Seer's portrait from the book. **Click it** (or **Choose the Seer's picture** while there is none) to pick another image.
- **What the book says**, read-only: the Seer's stat line (VIG, CLA, SPI and GD, hover each for its name) and the description under it, shown whole.
- **Notes** is yours: a rich-text box for what your Knight knows of their Seer, what they've asked them, what they owe. Click the edit button in its corner to write.

The book's part fills itself in whenever the page is drawn for someone who can edit the Knight:

- If **Knighted by** is empty, it is filled with the Seer of the Knight the **Known as** box names.
- If you type another of the book's Seers into **Knighted by**, the picture and description switch to that Seer.
- A picture or description you chose yourself is never replaced, and your Notes are never touched.
- A name the book doesn't know fills in nothing; until the rulebook is imported, the page says it will be filled in once it is.

The page holds the Seer's numbers only for reference: nothing on it rolls, and the Seer's harm isn't kept here. If the Seer joins a fight, the Referee makes them an NPC (below) and keeps their harm on that sheet.

A Squire has no Seer page: nobody has knighted them yet. When they're Knighted, choosing their Knight names their Seer. See [Choosing a Knight](Choosing-a-Knight#when-a-squire-is-knighted).

## The Seer in the Choose a Knight window

When you pick a Knight in **Choose a Knight**, the right-hand side ends with **Knighted by**: the Seer's portrait, name and the lines the book gives about them (not their numbers), so you know who knighted you before you choose. See [Choosing a Knight](Choosing-a-Knight#the-choose-a-knight-window).

## Making the Seer an NPC (Referee)

On a Knight's Seer page the Referee also sees **Make this Seer**. It brings the Seer into the world as an NPC of their own, built from the stat block on their Knight's page, and opens their sheet. If that Seer has been made already, it opens the one that exists rather than making a second. A Seer whose stat block the book doesn't give (or Import PDF hasn't read) can't be made, and you're told so.

Every Seer is also in the **NPCs** compendium, under the **Seers** folder, one sub-folder per d6 result, each with their portrait and a note saying which Knight they knighted. Drag one into the world when you need them. See [NPCs, Structures and Compendiums](NPCs-Structures-and-Compendiums).

## The Seer at a Sanctum (Referee)

A Sanctum on the Realm map is home to a Seer. In the Places window's Referee part for a Sanctum hex, the **Seer** section has:

- **Roll the Seer** (or **Roll Another Seer**): rolls d6 and d12 on the Knights table for the Seer who lives there.
- **Choose the Seer**: opens the **Choose the Seer** window to pick one by hand.
- **Make this Seer**: makes the Seer living there an NPC, as above.

![The Choose the Seer window, with The Company's Seers at the top (each card saying which Knight they knighted), then Every Other Seer by d6 result with a search box, one Seer picked and the Make Them This Sanctum's Seer button](images/the-seer-chooser.webp)

In **Choose the Seer**:

- **The Company's Seers** come first: the Seers who knighted the players' Knights, each card saying who they knighted.
- **Every Other Seer** follows, one d6 result at a time. **Click a number** to show that result, or **type in the search box** to find a Seer by name or roll from any result.
- A card marked **Lives here** is this Sanctum's Seer now; one living at another Sanctum says where.
- **Click a card** to pick them, then **Make Them This Sanctum's Seer**. A Seer can live at more than one Sanctum; the window tells you if they already do.

Until the rulebook is imported, the Seers go by their roll alone.
