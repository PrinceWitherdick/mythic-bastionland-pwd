# The Knight Sheet

The Knight sheet is laid out after the printed character sheet: your Knight's name lines, portrait and heraldry across the top, Virtues, Guard and Glory down the left, and Ability, Passion, Feats, Gambits and Scars down the right. Everything a player rolls in play starts here. A chosen Knight's window is titled the way the book styles them, such as "Isolde the Silk Knight".

A brand new Knight opens on an empty page until they're chosen. See [Choosing a Knight](Choosing-a-Knight).

## The pages

![Dame Isolde Marrow's sheet on the Knight page, with the portrait, name lines, verse and painted shield across the top, the Attack, Duel, Take Damage and Luck Roll bar, Virtues with current and maximum boxes, Conditions, Rank and Age pills o](images/the-knight-sheet-knight-tab.webp)

The pages are an icon rail hanging off the window's right edge (it moves to the left edge when the window is too close to the right of your screen). Hover an icon to see its name.

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Knight** | Virtues, Guard, Glory, Conditions, Recovery, Age, Rank, Successor, Ability, Passion, Feats, Gambits, Scars and the Oath. The sheet opens here. |
| **Property, Steed and Squire** | Your steed, your Squire and everything you carry. Its icon shows what carries your things: a horse while you ride a steed, your Squire while one serves you, a backpack otherwise. See [Property, Steeds and Squires](Property-Steeds-and-Squires). |
| **Seer** | Who knighted you, their picture and description, and your notes on them. See [The Seer](The-Seer). |
| **Travels** | Where the Company has been on the Realm map. See [Travels and Places](Travels-and-Places). |
| **Timeline** | This Knight's thread of the campaign Timeline. See [The Campaign Timeline](The-Campaign-Timeline). |
| **Chronicle** | Your own notes, as a page of rich text. |
| **Settings** | Your own display settings. Shown only on your own Knight. See [Your Settings and Accessibility](Your-Settings-and-Accessibility). |

A Squire's sheet has fewer pages: their own **Squire** page in place of Knight, Property and Seer, then Travels, Timeline, Chronicle and Settings. See [Property, Steeds and Squires](Property-Steeds-and-Squires#the-squire-sheet).

## The header

- The sheet has three name lines: **They were named**, then **Known as the ... Knight**, then **Their ultimate fate was**. Type straight into them. The Known as box refuses a Knight another living character already is (see [Choosing a Knight](Choosing-a-Knight#no-two-characters-are-the-same-knight)).
- **The verse** from the Knight's page in the book sits under the name lines, as long as the Known as box still names that Knight. It is read from your imported rulebook.
- **Click the portrait** to open it larger (see [The portrait window](#the-portrait-window)). Hover it to see a larger copy without opening anything. With no picture yet, a click goes straight to choosing one.
- **Click the shield** to paint your heraldry. See [Heraldry](Heraldry).
- **Click the small ? beside the title** to open the **Knighthood** window: the book's Knighthood and Glory pages laid out in five sections (Knighthood, Virtues, Squire, Glory, Rank), with your present rank picked out. It reopens at the section you read last, and offers **Read pp6–7** in the rulebook reader once the book is imported.

Under the header is the action bar, shown to anyone who can edit the Knight:

- **Attack** opens the Attack dialog. See [Combat for Players](Combat-for-Players).
- **Duel** challenges the one Token you have targeted to a duel. See [Combat for Players](Combat-for-Players#duels).
- **Take Damage** opens the Damage dialog. See [Combat for Players](Combat-for-Players#taking-damage).
- **Luck Roll** asks the odds and rolls the Luck die. See [Saves, Luck and Feats](Saves-Luck-and-Feats#the-luck-roll).

### The title bar buttons

The window's title bar carries a few buttons left of Foundry's own:

- **New Knight** rolls or picks a Knight from the book onto this sheet again, replacing their scores, Property, Ability and Passion (Scars and the Chronicle stay). On a Squire it reads **Knight this Squire**, and on a Squire just Knighted who hasn't chosen yet, **Choose Their Knight**. See [Choosing a Knight](Choosing-a-Knight).
- **Domain** opens the Domain this Knight rules, and reads its name once there is one. Until then it is drawn faded and offers to found one; you can also drag a Domain from the Actors tab onto the sheet. See [Domains and Courts](Domains-and-Courts).
- **Ledger** opens every change ever made to the Knight. See [The Ledger](#the-ledger).

## The Knight page

### Virtues, Guard, Armour and Glory

- **Click a Virtue's name** (Vigour, Clarity or Spirit) to roll a Save against it. See [Saves, Luck and Feats](Saves-Luck-and-Feats).
- Each Virtue and Guard has two boxes: the current score and, smaller, its maximum. Type in either.
- **Hover the shield badge** (A1, A2 ...) beside the Virtues to see which pieces of armour make up the total and which don't count right now. Armour is set on the Property page.
- **Glory** is a box you type in. Rank follows it.

### Conditions and Recovery

**Conditions** shows a pill for each condition. The ones you can switch (Fatigued, Exposed, Wounded, Mortal Wound, Slain, Mounted) are buttons; Exhausted and Impaired light up by themselves from your scores. Afflictions, dice you hold for the fight, and marks left by Gambits show here too, each with an × to clear it. Hover a pill to read what it means.

**Recovery** lists how each Virtue and Guard comes back, with a button to restore each and a mug to **Rest**. See [Saves, Luck and Feats](Saves-Luck-and-Feats#recovery-and-rest).

### Age and Rank

- **Click an Age** (Young, Mature, Old) to change it. Growing older opens **Growing Older**, which offers **Roll Virtues** (each Virtue rolled again on d12+d6, the higher kept on turning Mature, the lower on turning Old) or **Just change Age**.
- **Rank** shows the five ranks with the Glory each needs, your own lit. Under it, your rank's "Worthy of..." line and how much more Glory reaches the next. Hover a rank to read what it is worthy of.

### Successor

The Knight or Squire named to follow this Knight.

- **Click Name a Successor** to pick from every other Knight and Squire in the world. Your own Squire is picked to start with.
- **Drag another Knight** from the Actors tab onto the sheet to name them. (A Squire dropped on a Knight is taken as their Squire instead; see [Property, Steeds and Squires](Property-Steeds-and-Squires#squires).)
- **Click the successor's name** to open their sheet, or the × to unname them.

See [Scars, Death and Successors](Scars-Death-and-Successors#successors).

### Ability, Passion, Feats and Gambits

- **Ability** and **Passion** are rows like any possession: click the name to show it in chat, and hover for the pencil that opens it. A Passion's row has a wine glass to indulge it and restore SPI. A Knight with no Ability or Passion has **+ Ability** / **+ Passion** buttons.
- **Feats** lists Smite, Focus and Deny. Clicking one only shows it to the table: each is really performed during an Attack. See [Saves, Luck and Feats](Saves-Luck-and-Feats#feats).
- **Gambits** lists every Gambit. Click one to post its rule to chat. See [Combat for Players](Combat-for-Players#gambits).

### Scars and the Oath

- **Scars** lists the Scars you carry. **Roll Scar** rolls one by hand, and **Patch Up** appears while a few moments' care would help. A Scar still waiting to be settled shows a faint hand icon. See [Scars, Death and Successors](Scars-Death-and-Successors).
- **Oath** is the oath every Knight swears, for reference.

## The portrait window

Clicking the portrait opens the whole picture in its own window. Its title bar has:

- **Change Picture**: choose another image (owners only).
- **Frame**: choose which part of the picture the sheet shows (owners only).
- **Show Players**: show the picture to everyone signed in (Referee only).

The window follows the Knight, so a picture changed anywhere shows here at once.

### Framing the portrait

**Frame** shows the whole picture with the part the sheet uses boxed, and a preview of the sheet's view beside it.

- **Drag inside the box** to move it, **drag a corner** to resize it, or **drag anywhere outside** to draw a new box.
- **Arrow keys** nudge the box; hold **Shift** for bigger steps.
- **+** and **−** make the box bigger or smaller.
- **Start Over** goes back to the default box. Nothing is saved until you press **Save**.

A moving picture (a video) can't be framed.

## The Ledger

![Dame Isolde Marrow's Ledger window, lines grouped under a date heading, each saying what changed, by whom and when, some with "via Damage" or "via a Scar", and the search box, filter menu and order menu along the top](images/the-knight-sheet-ledger.webp)

The **Ledger** button opens a list of every change made to this Knight, grouped by day, each line saying what changed, who changed it and when, and often what caused it ("via Damage", "via a Scar", "via growing older").

- **Type in the search box** to find lines.
- **Pick from the filter menu** to see one kind of change (Virtues and Guard, Conditions, Property and so on) or one subject within it.
- **Switch the order** between **Newest first** and **Oldest first**.
- **Click the pencil** (owners only) to tick lines and **Delete** them. Deleting more than one line asks first. It can't be undone.

## The Chronicle

The **Chronicle** page is a rich-text page for your notes. Click the edit button in its corner to write, and save with the editor's save button. The editor's font menu offers the system's own typefaces, and headings come out in the sheet's capitals unless you choose another font.

## Small things worth knowing

- **Sheets reopen where you left them.** Knight sheets you had open when you reloaded come back in the same place, on the same page. Turn this off with **Reopen Sheets on Reload** in your settings.
- **Knight Tokens show their name on hover to everyone**, so the whole table knows who is who. A display setting chosen by hand on the Token is left alone.
- **Someone who can only view your Knight** can still click your item names, Feats and Gambits to post them to chat, and open your Knight's table to read it.
- **The Property page keeps its order.** Dragging a possession among things of another kind snaps it back and says why. See [Property, Steeds and Squires](Property-Steeds-and-Squires#the-property-list).
