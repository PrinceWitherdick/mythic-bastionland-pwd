# Myths and Omens

A Realm holds six Myths, each with six Omens met in order. The system keeps track of which Myths the Realm holds, where they lie and how far each has got, and gives the Referee one page to run them from: the **Myths and Omens** tab of the [Referee Toolkit](The-Referee-Toolkit). This page covers that tab, **The Realm's Myths** window where you settle which six the Realm holds, Myths you write yourself, resolving and replacing a Myth, and the City Quest.

Everything here is for Referees only. Players never see the Myths page, the Myths window or a written Myth.

## The Myths and Omens tab

Open the Referee Toolkit and pick the dragon icon on its tab rail. The page shows the Realm chosen in the Toolkit's banner. With no Realm Scene yet, it tells you to roll one first (see [Making a Realm](Making-a-Realm)).

Each Myth is folded to one row, so all six fit without scrolling:

- its picture, its number and name, and the book's verse for it run into one italic line (hover the line to read it whole);
- six pips, filled for each Omen met, with the one playing out in red; hover them for "Omens seen: N of 6";
- chips: the hex it lies in, **Hidden from players** while its hex is still hidden on the map, and **Resolved** once it is over.

**Click a row** to open that Myth. Opening one folds whichever was open before.

**Click the hex chip** (**Show on the Map**) to view the Realm, pan to the hex, ring it in green and ping it for you alone, and open that hex in Places. No player sees the ping, so a hidden Myth stays hidden.

Names, verses, Omens, tables and Casts come from your own rulebook through Import PDF (see [Bringing In Your Rulebook](Bringing-In-Your-Rulebook)). Until then a Myth goes by its roll and each Omen by its number, and the page says so.

## Marking Omens

An open Myth lists its six Omens in order. The Omen playing out is labelled **Current** and the one to come **Next**; both are written out in full. Every other Omen is cut to one line.

- **Click the Omen the Company has met** (the row or its numbered disc). It becomes Current, and every Omen before it is crossed out. You can jump ahead: clicking Omen 4 marks 1 to 4 met.
- **Click the Current Omen again** to take it back, leaving the one before it playing out. That is how a wrong click is undone.

Marking an Omen posts nothing to chat. It does write the Omen met on the [Campaign Timeline](The-Campaign-Timeline), at the hex where the Company stands.

You rarely need to mark Omens by hand: the Wilderness Roll counts them itself when it calls for one (see [Exploring the Wilderness](Exploring-the-Wilderness)). The Places window's Lay of the Land, and **Edit this hex**, also have minus and plus buttons for the Omens of the Myth in that hex. All of them change the same count.

Once all six are met, the card says so.

## Scenes linked to an Omen

If you have a Scene ready for an Omen, such as a map of where it plays out, link it to that Omen so it's at hand when the Company gets there.

- **Drag a Scene** from the Scenes sidebar onto an Omen, here or on the sheet of a Myth you wrote. It shows under the Omen as a pill with its name. An Omen can have several.
- **Click the pill** to activate the Scene, taking everyone there.
- **Click the eye** to view the Scene yourself, without moving the players.
- **Click the cross** to unlink it.

When a Wilderness Roll brings that Omen, its card has an **Activate** button for each Scene linked to it.

A book Myth keeps its links only as long as it holds that Myth: roll it again, or replace it, and the new one starts with none. A Myth you wrote keeps its links with it. A Scene still in a compendium can't be linked, so import it into the world first. A Scene you delete drops off by itself.

## Picture, verse and table

- **Click the Myth's picture** (on the folded row too) to open it larger in a window of its own, with the verse under it. The eye button in that window's title bar, **Show Players**, shows the picture to everyone signed in, with the Myth's name and verse. Use it when the Company meets the Myth; it reveals the name.
- **The Myth's table** sits in a fold under the Cast. It opens by itself when the Current Omen points to it.
- **Click "see opposite"** inside an Omen to open the table and scroll to it, without marking that Omen.
- **Roll the Table** (in the toolbar, or the die at the table's top left) rolls a d6 for each column. **Click a column's heading** to roll that column alone. A highlight steps down each column and stops on the row rolled, and a card with the result is whispered to the Referees.

If your world was imported before the tables were read, the Toolkit reads the table from your rulebook as you open the Myth ("Reading this Myth's table from your rulebook..."). With no rulebook at all, it asks you to give the world one or run Import PDF.

## The Cast

Under the Omens, **The Cast** folds the people printed for the Myth, with a count of how many you have made (for example "The Cast, 1 of 3 made").

- **Make Them** creates the person as an actor with the stat block the book prints, wearing the Myth's picture, in an Actors folder named after the Myth. You can rename or move that folder; the system still finds it.
- Four of the book's Cast are only ever met as a warband, though their names don't say so. They are made as Warbands, and any already in your world are set to the Warband scale. See [Warbands in combat](Running-Combat#warbands-in-combat).
- Once made, the person's name sits under their entry. **Click the name** to open their sheet, or **click the cross** to take them out of the Cast.
- **Drag any actor from the Actors directory onto a Myth's card** (open or folded) to count them among its Cast. They show with a **Yours** chip. An actor still in a compendium can't join: import it into the world first.

A Cast belongs to the Myth, not to its number, so actors made for a book Myth join it again in any Realm that holds it. The book's NPCs, Casts included, are also in the **NPCs** compendium, and one dragged from there into the world joins its Myth's Cast here (see [NPCs, Structures and Compendiums](NPCs-Structures-and-Compendiums)).

## Your notes

**Your notes on this Myth** is a box at the foot of each open Myth, saved as you type. The notes belong to that Myth, so if it is replaced, the new Myth starts with an empty box, and undoing the replacement brings the old notes back.

## Resolving a Myth

When the table agrees a Myth is over, however it ended:

1. **Click Myth Resolved** at the foot of its card.
2. A window asks which Knights played a part. Tick them and press **Award Glory**: each gains 1 Glory and a card says so. The Myth is marked resolved whether or not you award any Glory.

A resolved Myth gets a **Resolved** chip, is written on the Timeline (with the Knights you ticked) and in the current Season's record on the Toolkit's **Time** page.

**Not Resolved** on the card undoes it: the mark, the Season's record and the Timeline entry all go.

## Replacing a resolved Myth

A resolved Myth is replaced by a new one in the next Season (the book's p27). You can do this three ways, all with the same result:

- **Roll the New Myth** on the resolved Myth's card;
- **Roll the New Myth** beside it under **Myths to Replace** on the Toolkit's **Time** page;
- the button **Roll a Myth to replace ...** on the card posted when the Season turns (see [Time, Phases and Seasons](Time-Phases-and-Seasons)). It disappears once pressed.

The new Myth is rolled with real dice on the Myths table, never one the Realm already holds. It takes the same number and the same hex, starts with no Omens met and is hidden from players. A card with its name and picture is whispered to the Referees. The Realm's **Undo** takes the roll back, old notes and all (see [Painting and Editing the Realm](Painting-and-Editing-the-Realm)).

## The Realm's Myths window

This is where you settle which six Myths the Realm holds, apart from play. Open it from:

- **Choose the Myths** in the Keep this Realm window, right after rolling a Realm;
- **Choose the Myths** under the six Myths on the Toolkit's Myths page;
- **Choose** beside the Myth's roll in **Edit this hex**, which opens the window on that Myth.

The left side, **In the Realm**, lists every Myth number with its Myth, hex and page. The right side, **The Myths table**, has the book's 72, a page for each d6 result (buttons **1** to **6**), a **Yours** page for Myths you wrote, and a search box that finds a Myth by name or roll across every page. Hover a picture on either side to see it larger.

- **Click a row on the left** to choose which number you are working on.
- **The die on a row** rolls that Myth again, on one the Realm hasn't got.
- **Roll them all again**, under the list, rerolls every Myth at once, no two alike. Written Myths are rerolled into book Myths too.
- **Pick a Myth on the right**, then press **Make it Myth N** to give it to the chosen number. A Myth the Realm already holds is marked with its number and can be looked at, but not taken twice.
- **The cross on a row** takes that Myth out of the Realm after you confirm (**Take It Out**). Its hex is left empty, its Omens met go with it, and the row shows **No Myth**.
- **The die on an empty row**, or **Make it Myth N** with that row chosen, puts a Myth back under that number in a remote hex of its own, hidden, as the Realm's Myths were placed when it was made.

In every other case only which Myth it is changes: the number and hex stay the same. A Myth given a new roll here starts with no Omens met. Nothing in this window posts to chat.

## Moving a Myth to another hex

To move a Myth, open the hex you want it in with **Edit this hex** (from **Edit hex** in the Places title bar, or the Inspect tool while drawing) and set **In this hex** to **Myth**. If every Myth number is already on the map, a **Move a Myth Here** window asks which one to move. It keeps its roll and its Omens met. Close the window without choosing and nothing changes. A number left free (one taken out in The Realm's Myths, for example) is used instead, with no question.

**Edit this hex** also sets a Myth's number and roll by hand, and its **Hidden from players** / **Revealed to players** button shows or hides the Myth on the players' map.

## Writing your own Myth

You can write a Myth of your own and make it one of a Realm's six. Each written Myth is a Journal entry, filed in a **Your Myths** folder, so it can be used in any Realm of the world.

Start one with:

- **New Myth** at the top of the Journal directory;
- **Write a Myth**, the first card on the **Yours** page of The Realm's Myths.

The sheet has a picture (click the frame to choose one; **No Picture** clears it), a **Name**, two lines of **Verse**, six **Omens**, a **Cast** note and an optional **Table** of two columns and six rows. Everything saves as you type. Write "see opposite" in an Omen to make it open the table on the Toolkit, as a book Myth's does. Close a new Myth without writing anything and its empty entry is deleted.

To use it, open The Realm's Myths, choose a number on the left, pick your Myth on the **Yours** page and press **Make it Myth N**. Written Myths are only ever chosen by hand; no roll lands on one.

On the Toolkit a written Myth works like a book Myth: Omens, picture and Show Players, table rolls, notes, Glory and the Timeline. Its Cast is whoever you drag onto it. **Edit this Myth** on its card (or under the pick in The Realm's Myths) opens the sheet again. Delete the Journal entry and the Realm's Myth falls back to being called "Myth N".

## The City Quest

The City Quest section sits at the foot of the Myths page, below the six Myths. It lists the Omens of the City met so far and says when the Quest ends, with its own Cast fold that works like a Myth's.

- **Roll an Omen of the City** rolls a d12, adds the Omens of the City already met, and takes the next unmet one from there. The card is whispered to the Referees.
- **Start Over** forgets every Omen of the City met, after you confirm, as for a new Company.

Once any player's Knight is a Knight Radiant, a Wilderness Roll that would bring a random Myth's Omen brings an Omen of the City instead, rolled for you. Once an Omen of 18 or higher has come up, the Quest is over and the roll button is greyed out.

## Playing solo

With **Solo play** on (see [Solo Play](Solo-Play)), the Myths page keeps the Realm's secrets from you too:

- A Myth none of whose Omens has been met is a row reading **A Myth not yet met**, with nothing to open.
- Once met, it is named, but only the Omens met so far are listed, and its hex chip appears only once the Company has stood in its hex.
- **Choose the Myths** is gone, and **Edit this hex** can't change what stands in a hex.
- A replacement Myth's card doesn't name it; it is known by its first Omen, like the others.
