# Your First Session

A walk-through for the Referee, from a brand-new world to the Company standing on its first Realm. Each step is short and links to the page that covers it properly.

## The Welcome window

A new world opens the **Welcome to Mythic Bastionland** window for the Referee the first time it loads. It shows only in a world that has never been set up and holds no Actors, Scenes or Journal entries, so a world already in play never sees it. Closing it once, by any means, stops it opening by itself again. To open it later, use **Open the Welcome** in Foundry's Configure Settings, or the **Welcome** button on the Referee Toolkit's Settings page.

![The Welcome to Mythic Bastionland window, with Your Rulebook on the left and The Company and Where Things Are on the right](images/bringing-in-your-rulebook-welcome.webp)

The window has two columns.

| Part | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Your Rulebook** | Bring in your own PDF of the book. See below. |
| **The Company** | How many Knights have followed the call, and **Knight Those Subjects** to make them. |
| **Solo Mode** | Tick it if you are playing alone. See [Solo Play](Solo-Play). |
| **Where Things Are** | A short list of where Knights, Realms, Sites and the Referee's tables are made. |
| **Skip the Tours** | Stops the two Realm Tours starting by themselves. |
| **Make Your First Realm** | Shown while the world has no Realm yet. Closes the Welcome and starts New Realm. |
| **Done** | Closes the window. |

## Bring in your rulebook

The system ships with none of the book. Under **Your Rulebook**, click **Choose the PDF** and pick your copy. The system keeps a copy to read in Foundry and imports the art and tables: every Knight's, Seer's and Myth's picture, the Spark Tables, and the compendiums of Arms and Goods. If another world on the same server already brought the PDF in, the Welcome says so and imports from that copy by itself, with nothing to choose.

You can do this later instead: **Import PDF** sits in slot 9 of your hotbar, and the **Rulebook** button in the Journal tab offers the same. Everything about this step is on [Bringing In Your Rulebook](Bringing-In-Your-Rulebook).

## The chat cards

A new world also whispers the Referees a few cards in chat:

- **Your Rulebook**, with an **Import PDF** button (left out when the book is already here).
- **Your First Realm**, with a **Create a Realm** button that starts New Realm.
- **Recommended Modules**, naming Dice So Nice!, Sequencer and JB2A, SoundFx Library, FXMaster and Tokenizer, with a **Manage Modules** button. It is left out when every one of them is already on. See [Installing and Recommended Modules](Installing-and-Recommended-Modules).

## The Company of Knights

Each player needs a Knight. You have two ways to give them one.

- **From the Welcome.** The count under **The Company** follows the players signed in who have no Knight yet. Step it with **-** and **+** (1 to 12) if you want more or fewer. **Knight Those Subjects** makes that many blank Knights, names each one for the player it is given to, and opens it on that player's screen so they can choose. Knights nobody is signed in for wait in the Actors tab; give one away with **Played by** on its sheet.
- **Players make their own.** By default, Players and Trusted Players may create Actors (you can take that back in Foundry's permissions), and a player's **Create Actor** offers a Knight only.

Either way, the player then rolls or picks their Knight from the book. See [Choosing a Knight](Choosing-a-Knight).

## Make the first Realm

Click **New Realm** at the top of the Scenes tab (or **Make Your First Realm** in the Welcome, or **Create a Realm** on its chat card). You are asked whether to **Roll it**, **Draw it myself**, or **Import a map I drew**. For a first session, rolling is quickest:

1. On **The Realm** page, give it a name. The seed can stay as it is.
2. On **The Company** page, leave **Place the Company on the map** ticked and choose the Company's **Start**: Wanderer, Courtier or Ruler. Pick a picture for the Company Token if you like.
3. Click **Roll the Realm**.
4. The **Keep this Realm?** window opens at the foot of the map. Click **Roll again** until you like what you see, then **Keep this Realm**.

The Realm Key, with every Myth, Holding and Seer, is whispered to the Referees. Players never see it. Every way of making a Realm is on [Making a Realm](Making-a-Realm).

## Placing the Company Token

One Token stands for the whole Company. It belongs to no Actor, so every player can move it.

- A **Courtier** Company starts at the Seat of Power by itself.
- For a **Wanderer** or **Ruler** Company, you are handed the Token to carry. A faint copy follows the pointer and snaps to the hex under it. **Left-click** a hex to stand it there. **Right-click** or press **Escape** to put it down later.
- Whenever a Realm has no Company Token, a **Place the Company** button stands over the top edge of the map for the Referee. Click it to carry the Company again.

If the Token is ever deleted, a card whispered to the Referees offers **Put them back**. More on [Travels and Places](Travels-and-Places#the-company-token).

## The two Realm Tours

The system uses Foundry's Tours to point at the buttons you need first.

- **Your First Realm** plays when you first close the Welcome, if no Realm exists yet. It opens the Scenes tab and points at **New Realm**.
- **The Realm's Buttons** plays once the world's first Realm is made. It points at the two buttons beside the sidebar: the footprints (**Mark the Hexes Visited**) and **Places**.

The second Tour waits until you have finished placing the Company, so its overlay never takes that click. Tick **Skip the Tours** in the Welcome to stop them starting by themselves; the choice is kept in this browser, for every world you open in it. Either Tour can be played at any time from Foundry's **Tour Management** window.

## What everyone sees on a Realm

Once the Realm is on screen:

- The **Phase, Season and Age** hang as one bar at the top of the screen for everyone. For a Referee, clicking it lets down **Next Phase**, **Weeks Pass**, **Months Pass**, **A Year Passes** and **End the Session**. See [Time, Phases and Seasons](Time-Phases-and-Seasons).
- **Travel** sits on the left of the map and **Rest and Exploration** on its right, with the rules and the Referee's roll buttons. See [Exploring the Wilderness](Exploring-the-Wilderness).
- The footprints and **Places** buttons stand just left of the sidebar. See [Places and the Lay of the Land](Places-and-the-Lay-of-the-Land) and, for players, [Travels and Places](Travels-and-Places).
- The crown in the scene controls holds the Referee's Realm tools. See [Painting and Editing the Realm](Painting-and-Editing-the-Realm).

| 2 | Rulebook | Places |

| 4 | Luck Roll | |

The **6** key opens the Spark Tables for a Referee. In a solo world the Referee also gets **Places** and the **Timeline**. Every macro and key is listed on [Macros and Hotkeys](Macros-and-Hotkeys).
