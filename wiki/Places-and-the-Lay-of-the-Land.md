# Places and the Lay of the Land

The **Places** window is the record of where the Company has been on a Realm: the same pages the players see, with nothing they haven't found. For the Referee it is also the one place to look at any hex of the Realm. Under a chosen hex sits its **Lay of the Land**, which only you see: your note on the hex, everything rolled there, and every roll and action the hex offers.

This page is the Referee's side. What players see is on [Travels and Places](Travels-and-Places).

## Opening Places

- **Places**, the map-and-pin button just left of the sidebar on a Realm.
- **Double-click open ground** on a Realm, with the Token tools in hand. A Referee can open any hex this way.
- **Double-click the Company Token** to open the hex it stands in.
- **Inspect hexes** in the Realm tools: click a hex.
- **A hex's row** on the Referee Toolkit's Places page. See [The Referee Toolkit](The-Referee-Toolkit).
- When the Company comes to rest in a hex, Places opens on it for you. The **Reaching a New Hex** setting (per browser) turns this off: choose **Nothing** instead of **Open the hex in Places**. An arrival on a Realm you aren't looking at waits until you look at it.

The window is the same one players have, wider for a Referee. A hint at the top reminds you that players see these same pages, and that only the Lay of the Land is yours alone.

## The three pages

The left side of the window has a **Realm** picker when there is more than one to choose, a **Search the places** box that narrows every page, and three pages. The page you last chose is remembered in your browser.

| Page | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Places** | Every hex the Company knows, in three lists: **Places of Note**, **Wilderness** and **Heard Of** (told of, written about or seen from afar, but not reached). |
| **Chart** | A drawn chart of the Realm as the Company knows it, with a key. |
| **Journey** | Where the Company went and what happened, by Season and Phase. |

On the **Places** page:

- **Show only** narrows the list: **All**, **Holdings**, **Landmarks**, **Myths**, **Told**, **Noted** or **Not Visited**. A Referee also gets **People**, for hexes with someone rolled up in them.
- **Order** sorts by **Last reached first**, **By name** or **Most visited first**.
- **Hover a row** to ring that hex on the map. The **crosshair** at the row's end shows the hex on the map, ringed in green until your next click.
- **Click a row** to open that hex on the right.

On the **Chart** page:

- **Click a hex** to open it.
- **Show the way the Company went** draws the route.
- **Ctrl and the mouse wheel** zoom about the pointer. **Double-click** the chart to zoom in there, and again to go back to whole. Once zoomed, **drag with either mouse button** to move about; a throw glides a little. **Whole chart** zooms back out.

On the **Journey** page, **click a line** to open its hex. **Hover a line** and an **×** appears at its end: forget that visit, that telling, that Barrier line, or the Company's note. A Barrier forgotten here stays on the map.

## The chosen hex

![The top of the Places hex pane with the pen beside the hex name on hover, the coordinates under it, the terrain and feature pills, the visits line with its eraser, and Edit hex, Journal and Ping in the window's title bar](images/places-and-the-lay-of-the-land-heading.webp)

The right side shows the hex chosen, first as the players know it, then its Lay of the Land.

- **The heading** is the hex's name, with its column and row under it. **Click the heading** to name the hex: **Enter** keeps the name, **Escape** leaves it as it was, and a blank name takes it away. Players see a name only for hexes in their own Places.
- **The visits line** says how often and when the Company came. **Count a Visit** marks an unvisited hex as visited. The **eraser** opens **Forget What's Kept Here** (see below).
- **The Company's Note** is the players' shared note on the hex. You can read and edit it.

Three buttons sit in the window's title bar:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit hex** | Opens Edit this hex, for the terrain, the Holding, Myth or Landmark in it, and its Barriers. See [Painting and Editing the Realm](Painting-and-Editing-the-Realm#edit-this-hex). |
| **Journal** | Opens the hex's Journal entry, making one if it has none. See [Hex journals](#hex-journals). |
| **Ping** | Shows the hex on the map, ringed in green until your next click. |

### Forget What's Kept Here

The eraser on the visits line opens a window with a tick box for each kind of thing the hex keeps, each with its count: **the Company's visits**, **Spark Table rolls**, **your note**, **what the players were told**, **Barriers met from here** and **the players' note**. Kinds the hex doesn't hold are left out. **Forget Everything Here** ticks them all, and the visits start ticked. Press **Forget**; this window is the only confirmation.

## The Lay of the Land

Under the players' part, **The Lay of the Land** is yours alone. It opens with the hex's terrain and what stands in it, including what players haven't found, and for a hex near a Holding, whose rule it lies under.

### What's here, and telling the players

**What's here** is your note on the hex. It saves as you leave the box.

A line under it says where the players stand:

- **The Company hasn't been told this yet**, ending in **Tell the players**.
- **Out of date**, when your note has changed since they were told, ending in **Update the players**.
- **The Company was told this**, when what they hold matches your note.

**Tell the players** sends the note as it stands. Players find it in their Places under **What the Referee Told You**, with when they were told. Each telling is kept, so emptying your note later doesn't take back what they already hold.

### The tabs

Under the note, a tab for each kind of thing that can be rolled in the hex. A tab shows only where the hex has one. Each tab shows its count, kept rolls come first, and its buttons sit at the foot. The tab you choose is kept as you move from hex to hex. The **arrow keys**, **Home** and **End** move between tabs.

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **People** | The people met here, each with a name, their traits and who they have heard of. **Roll a Person** opens the Roll a Person window. |
| **The Land** | Everything rolled for the land, each go side by side with its date. **Roll the Land** rolls the land, its weather and one feature on the Nature Spark Tables and keeps them. Then **Wilderness Roll here**, **Ask Somebody**, **Search the Hex** and **Look from a Vantage Point**. |
| **Landmark** (named by its kind) | The Landmark's **Name**, with a die to rename it, and what the Landmark offers. A Sanctum also has its Seer. |
| **Myth** | The Myth and its page, the Omens seen with **-** and **+**, its Cast, **Open in the Toolkit**, and **Revealed** or **Hidden**. |
| **Holding** | Who rules it, the Holding's rolls, **Roll Up This Holding**, **Local Mood**, and **Grant to a Knight**. |

**Roll a Person**, **Roll the Land** and **Roll Up This Holding** appear once Import PDF has read the Spark Tables from your book; until then a line says so.

![Places on a visited wilderness hex, its The Land tab with one go of kept rolls (Feature, Weather and Land) and its pencil, and Roll the Land, Wilderness Roll here, Ask Somebody, Search the Hex and Look from a Vantage Point](images/places-and-the-lay-of-the-land-tabs.webp)

#### Changing what was rolled

- **Hover a go** of rolls and click its **pencil** to open **Edit These Rolls**. Reword any roll (leave it empty to go back to what was rolled), tick **Forget this roll**, or press **Forget all of them**.
- **Hover a person** and click their **pencil** to open **Edit This Person**: their name, each trait, who they have heard of, or **Forget this person**.
- **Hover a person's name** and click it to rename them in place.

#### Landmark

- Type a **Name**, or press the **die** (**Name it from a random page**) to take the prompt a random page of your book prints for this kind of Landmark. A line under it says which page it came from.
- The Landmark's offers, as buttons: a Monument's **Spend a Phase: Restore SPI**, a Hazard's **Push Through** and **Go Back**, a Ruin's **Echo a Myth**. See [Exploring the Wilderness](Exploring-the-Wilderness#landmarks).
- A **Sanctum** shows its Seer, with **Roll the Seer**, **Choose the Seer** (pick any of the book's Seers, with your players' Knights' Seers on top), and **Make this Seer** to bring them into the world as an NPC.

#### Myth

The Omens seen, the current and next Omen, the Myth's Cast as pills (one already made opens its sheet), **Open in the Toolkit** for the Myth's full card, and **Revealed** or **Hidden** to show its hex to the players. Which Myth stands here is changed in Edit this hex. See [Myths and Omens](Myths-and-Omens).

#### Holding

The **Ruler** row names the Knight and the Domain that rule it; each opens its sheet. **Grant to a Knight** (or **Grant to Another Knight**) picks a Knight to rule it: one with a Domain has that Domain rule it, one without founds a Domain here. Any of the book's Knights can be chosen; one not yet in the world is made as an NPC Knight with a Domain. See [Domains and Courts](Domains-and-Courts).

## Roll a Person

**Roll a Person** on the People tab opens a window of the People Spark Tables, all rolled as it opens.

- **Reroll All** rolls every table again.
- **Click a table's die** to reroll that table, or **a column's heading** to reroll that column.
- **Click an entry** to choose it instead of what was rolled. Click it again to let it go.
- **Save** keeps the person in the hex with a name nobody else here has, whispers you a card, and closes.

Nothing is kept until you save.

## The Spark Tables

The **Spark Tables** window holds every Spark Table from your book. Open it with **Spark Tables** in the Roll Tables tab, or press **6** (Referees only; press it again to close). It has five pages: **Wilderness Hex**, **Nature**, **Civilisation**, **People** and **Combat**.

![The Spark Tables window on the Nature page, with Animate selection and Keep rolls in the Company's hex ticked, Roll a Person, and the Land, Sky, Water, Weather, Flora and Fauna tables with a die by each](images/places-and-the-lay-of-the-land-spark-tables.webp)

- **Roll** a table for a d12 in each column. A highlight walks the table and lands on the result, and the roll is posted to chat in your current roll mode.
- **Animate selection** turns the highlight off or on, in this browser.
- **Keep rolls in** (in the header) keeps each roll in a hex: the one chosen in Places, or with Places closed, the hex the Company stands in. It is ticked each time the window opens. Untick it to roll without keeping.
- The **Wilderness Hex** page sets out the tables for filling a wilderness hex side by side. **Roll** rolls them all, a table's die rolls that table, a heading rolls that column, and a click on an entry chooses it. Nothing is kept until **Save to** the hex.
- The **People** page has its own **Roll a Person**, which rolls every People table at once.

## The first-arrival roll

The first time the Company comes to rest in a Wilderness hex with nothing written or rolled there, its land and one feature are rolled on the Nature Spark Tables, kept in the hex, and whispered to the Referees. Holdings are left alone. Turn this off with the world setting **Roll a New Hex's Land**.

## Hex journals

Each hex that has something rolled, written or named for it gets a Journal entry, in a folder named for the Realm. Any other hex gets one when you press **Journal** on it.

| Page | Who sees it |
| --- | --- |
| **What the Company Knows** | Players can read it once the hex is in their Places. |
| **What's Here** | You alone. A copy of your **What's here** note. |

The entries are written one way: edits made in the journal are lost the next time the hex changes. A notice at the top of each page says so, for its editors only, with a **Lay of the Land** button that opens the hex in Places. Turn the entries off with the world setting **Journal Entries for Hexes**.

## Settings

| Setting | Scope | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reaching a New Hex** | Your browser | Open the hex in Places when the Company comes to rest, or do nothing. |
| **Roll a New Hex's Land** | World | The first-arrival roll above. |
| **Journal Entries for Hexes** | World | A Journal entry for each hex. |

All three are also on the Referee Toolkit's Settings page. See [World Settings Reference](World-Settings-Reference).
