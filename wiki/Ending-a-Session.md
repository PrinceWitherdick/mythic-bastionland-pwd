# Ending a Session

**Ending a Session** is a Referee-only window that walks the book's end-of-session procedure (p16) in order: settle how much time passes, see what becomes of anything left hanging, catch up on Crisis Rolls and Glory, ask the players what they plan next, and write a line about the session. One press of **End the Session** at the foot of the window then moves the calendar on, keeps your notes and posts a summary card to chat.

![The Ending a Session window with Months picked under How much time passes, Add a situation, the Crisis Roll still owed, the Glory buttons, and the Plans and ambitions and This session boxes filled in](images/ending-a-session-window.webp)

## Opening the window

Only a Referee can open it. There are three ways in:

- **The hotbar.** Every Referee gets an **End the Session** macro in the slot labelled **0** at the end of the hotbar. Players don't get it. See [Macros and Hotkeys](Macros-and-Hotkeys).
- **The Phase banner.** Click any part of the calendar banner at the top of the screen to let down the **Move Time On** buttons: **Next Phase**, **Weeks Pass**, **Months Pass**, **A Year Passes** and **End the Session**. See [Time, Phases and Seasons](Time-Phases-and-Seasons).
- **The Referee Toolkit.** The **Time** page has an **End the Session** button among the ways of moving time on. See [The Referee Toolkit](The-Referee-Toolkit).

There is only ever one window. Opening it again while it is open brings the same window forward, so a session can't be ended twice at once.

## The steps

The window is a column of sections. Some only appear when they have something to do.

| Section | When it shows | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **How much time passes?** | Always | Pick **None**, **Weeks**, **Months** or **Years**, or roll for it. |
| **Anything left unresolved?** | Only when **Months** or **Years** is picked | Name situations left hanging and roll for each. |
| **Crisis Rolls still owed** | Only when a Domain hasn't made this Season's Crisis Roll | A **Crisis Roll** button per Domain. |
| **Glory** | Always | Award Glory won by other means than Rank. |
| **Plans and ambitions** | Always | What the players mean to do next time. |
| **This session** | Always | Your own recap of the session. |

### How much time passes?

The group decides, and you click the matching pill. Hover a pill to see what it means.

- **None**: nothing moves. The next session picks up where this one stopped.
- **Weeks**: on to the Season's next event, exactly as the **Weeks Pass** button does. If the next event is the Realm's collection at the Season's end, this turns the Season instead.
- **Months**: the Season turns, as **Months Pass** does.
- **Years**: the Age turns and the new one begins in Spring, as **A Year Passes** does.

**Click Uncertain** when the group can't agree. It rolls the Passage of Time table (the card goes to chat) and the window shows the result under the pills and picks the step for you:

- If the die says time turns now, the window picks **Months**, or **Years** when it is Winter, since a new Age starts in Spring.
- If the die says time turns after the next session, the window picks **None** and remembers the promise. The next time anyone opens the window, it already has **Months** (or **Years**) picked, with a line at the top saying a roll last session called for it.
- Otherwise it picks **None**.

You can still click a different pill after rolling. Picking a step other than the one the die chose drops any promise the roll made.

### Anything left unresolved?

This section only appears for **Months** and **Years**, since unresolved situations change as the Season or Age turns.

- **Click Add a situation** for a new row, and type what was left hanging.
- **Click Roll** on a row to roll the Unresolved Situation table for it. The card goes to chat, and the result shows under the row.
- **Click the ×** to take a row off the list.

Only rows you rolled make it onto the summary card. A rolled row with no name is listed as "A situation left unresolved".

### Crisis Rolls still owed

Lists each Domain that hasn't made its Crisis Roll this Season, so it can be made before the Season turns. **Click Crisis Roll** to make it there and then, just as on the Domain sheet. The Domain drops off the list once it has rolled. See [Domains and Courts](Domains-and-Courts).

### Glory

Three buttons: **Myth Resolved**, **Tournament Won** and **Battle Won**. Hover one for when it applies. Each opens the usual Glory window: tick the Knights who earned it and they each gain 1 Glory. That award posts its own card straight away, and a **Tournament Won** or **Battle Won** is written on the [Timeline](The-Campaign-Timeline) too. The window then lists the names beside the button, and the award goes on the summary card too.

### Plans and ambitions, and This session

Two text boxes. What you type stays put while you roll and award things in the window above.

Under **This session**, the box **Write it on the Timeline too, for everyone to read** is ticked to start with. It only appears when the world has the Campaign Timeline turned on.

## Pressing End the Session

The button at the foot of the window does nothing until a step is picked under **How much time passes?** (it warns you instead). Once one is, it:

1. **Moves time on.** Weeks, Months and Years do exactly what their own buttons do. Months and Years open the usual window for choosing who takes part in the turn and their pursuits.
2. **Writes your notes** into the notes of the Season that was just played, on the Referee Toolkit's **Time** page. They go under a heading such as "Session · Morning, Spring, Age 1", after anything already written there: your recap first, then the players' plans on a line starting "Next session:". If the Season has turned, that Season is now in the **Past Seasons** list further down the page, and the notes are there. Nothing is written if both boxes are empty.
3. **Remembers a promise** from the Passage of Time roll for next time, or forgets the one this session took up.
4. **Adds a row titled A Session Played** to the Company's thread on the [Campaign Timeline](The-Campaign-Timeline), dated to the Season that was played.
5. **Posts an Ending a Session card** to chat, dated with the calendar as it now stands.
6. **Closes the window.**

If the time step can't be carried out, nothing else happens and the window stays open with everything still in it. That covers closing the Season-turn window without confirming it, and pressing **Weeks** when the Season has no events left.

## Who sees what

| What | Where it goes | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passage of Time and Unresolved Situation rolls | A chat card each, as you roll | Whoever your chat mode lets see it |
| Glory awards | A chat card each, as you award; Tournament and Battle awards on the Timeline too | Whoever your chat mode lets see it; the Timeline is for everyone |
| The summary card | Chat, when the session ends. It lists how much time passed, each rolled situation and its result, the Glory awarded, and the players' plans | Whoever your chat mode lets see it |
| Your recap | The Season's notes on the Referee Toolkit; on the Timeline as well if the share box is ticked | The Toolkit is the Referee's alone. The Timeline is for everyone |
| The players' plans | The Season's notes, the summary card and the Timeline row | Everyone who sees the card or the Timeline |

The recap is never on the chat card. Unticking the share box keeps it off the Timeline, but the Timeline row still says how much time passed and what the players plan next.

> **Did you know?**
> - Closing the window without pressing **End the Session** throws away what you typed and the situations you listed. Anything already rolled or awarded stays done: the cards are in chat and the Glory is on the sheets.
> - The Timeline row is written even while the world has the Timeline turned off, so it is there if you turn the Timeline back on.
> - The window remembers only one thing between sessions: a turn the Passage of Time die put off until next time. Ending the next session uses that promise up, whichever step you pick then.
