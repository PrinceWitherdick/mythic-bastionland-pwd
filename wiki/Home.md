# Mythic Bastionland for Foundry VTT

An unofficial Foundry VTT system for [Mythic Bastionland](https://bastionlandpress.com/collections/all), Chris McDowall's game of Knights, Myths and the Realms they wander, made with his permission. It takes the bookkeeping off the table: Knights chosen from the book and rolled from their sheets, Attacks and Gambits on chat cards, a Realm map you roll or draw, the Company's travels with what the players were told, Myths and Omens, the Phases, Seasons and Ages, Domains and their Courts, and a Timeline of the whole campaign.

No book text or art ships with the system. Point it at your own PDF of the book and it fills itself in: see [Bringing In Your Rulebook](Bringing-In-Your-Rulebook).

This wiki covers what the system does and how to use it, including a lot that's easy to miss. If you only read one page, read [Hidden Tricks](Hidden-Tricks).

## Ten things most people miss

1. **Press `B`** to open the rulebook inside Foundry, and **click any page number** such as "(p16)" to jump there.
2. **Hover a bold rule word** on a card or sheet, such as Exposed or VIG, to see what it means.
3. **Drag an NPC onto your Knight's sheet** to make it your steed, or another Knight to name them your successor.
4. **Hover a hex chip** on a chat card to ring that hex on the map; click it to take your own view there.
5. **Double-click open ground on the Realm** to open that hex in Places. Double-click the Company's Token for the hex it stands in.
6. **Right-click the footprints button** beside the sidebar to choose how visited hexes are marked.
7. **Click the time bar** at the top of the screen (Referee) for Next Phase, Weeks Pass, Months Pass, A Year Passes and End the Session.
8. **Press `C`** (Referee) to open the Referee Toolkit, and **`6`** for the Spark Tables.
9. **`Shift`-click Apply Damage** on an Attack card to open the Take Damage window instead of taking the card's Damage as it stands.
10. **Your Settings tab** (last on your own Knight's rail) has colour schemes, text size, typeface and no-italics, for your screen only.
