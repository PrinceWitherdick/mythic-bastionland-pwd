# Domains and Courts

A **Domain** is an actor for a Holding someone rules (the book's p20 to p21). Its sheet keeps the ruler and their successor, the Holding on the Realm map, the Council and the tasks it has in hand, the Court, the Crises the Domain faces, its Grand Designs and the Warbands it has raised. The buttons along its top make the Domain's rolls: the Crisis Roll, Increased Collections and Drama in Court. A Knight who rules a Domain gets a button for it on their own sheet.

## Getting a Domain

There are several ways a Domain comes about. Whichever you use, the Domain is an ordinary actor in the Actors tab afterwards.

- **From a Knight's sheet.** Every Knight (not a Squire) has a **Domain** button in the sheet's title bar, drawn dimmed while they rule nothing. Click it to found one. If a Domain already names that Knight as its **Ruler** and no other Knight rules it, you are asked first whether to **Rule** that Domain or **Found a New Domain**.
- **The Found a Domain window** asks for the **Holding** it rules (any Holding on any Realm map, or **None on the map yet**), a **Name** (left blank, it takes the Holding's name, or "*Knight*'s Domain" with no Holding), and which other Knights sit **In the Circle**. Press **Found It**. The Domain is made in the Knight's folder, the Knight is written in as its ruler, and a Referee founding it gives it the same owners as the Knight. Founding needs permission to create actors; a player without it is told to ask the Referee.
- **Drag a Domain onto a Knight's sheet** from the Actors tab to make it the one they rule. A Domain with no ruler written in takes the Knight's name. Dragging from a compendium is refused.
- **From the map.** On a Holding's hex, the Referee's part of the Places window has a **Ruler** line with **Grant to a Knight**. See [Places and the Lay of the Land](Places-and-the-Lay-of-the-Land). A Knight of the book's table who isn't in the world yet is made as an NPC Knight with a Domain ruling the Holding.
- **From the Company's Start.** When a Realm is made for a **Ruler** Start, the Referee is asked **Who Rules?** and the Found a Domain window opens with the rest of the Company ticked into the Circle. A **Courtier** Start seats the Knights as Courtiers in the Court of the Domain ruling the Seat of Power, making that Domain if none rules it. If the Knights aren't made yet, the Referee gets a whispered card with **Seat the Knights at Court** for later. See [Making a Realm](Making-a-Realm).
- **By hand.** Create an actor of type **Domain** in the Actors tab.

## The sheet

The title is the Domain's name, with "Domain" added if the name doesn't already end in it. The pages are on a tab rail hanging off the window's edge, the same as the Knight sheet's (see [The Knight Sheet](The-Knight-Sheet)):

| Page | What is on it |
| --- | --- |
| **Rule** | Lordship, Authority (Warbands), the Council and its tasks, and **Pass On** and **Seize by Force** at the foot. The sheet opens here. |
| **Court and Crises** | The Crises, Grand Designs, and the Court. |
| **Timeline** | The Domain's own thread of the campaign Timeline. Gone when the world has the Timeline turned off. See [The Campaign Timeline](The-Campaign-Timeline). |
| **Notes** | A rich-text page that fills the window. |

The header and toolbar stay above every page. The header holds the Domain's arms, its name, and a **Seat of Power** tick box. Tick it for the Holding the whole Realm is ruled from: it raises the Warband limit from 2 to 3 and changes what Seize by Force asks.

The toolbar only shows to someone who can edit the Domain:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Crisis Roll** | Rolls for new Crises. |
| **Add Crisis** | Pick a Crisis yourself, for example one a story event brings. |
| **Increased Collections** | The Steward presses the Vassals for more. |
| **Drama in Court** | Rolls how this Season's Court drama draws the ruler in. |

### The Domain's arms

The square at the head of the sheet shows the ruling Knight's heraldry as a shield. With no ruler, or a ruler with no arms painted, the shield is blank. Hover it to see whose arms they are.

- **Click the shield** to choose what it shows: **The ruler's arms** (it follows whoever rules), **Another Knight's arms** (picked from a list; choosing from the list ticks the option for you), or **A picture**, which opens the file picker and shows the image square instead of a shield.
- The shield redraws by itself while the sheet is open when a Knight's arms, name or Domain change.
- The ruling Knight is the one linked to the Domain, or else the Knight whose name matches the **Ruler** field. A chosen Knight who is deleted, or a picture that is cleared, falls back to the ruler's arms.

How to paint arms is on [Heraldry](Heraldry).

### Arms on the Realm map

A Holding a Domain rules flies that Domain's arms on the Realm map, as a small shield at the top right of its icon, the way a lord's banner flew over the gate. It shows whatever the sheet's header shows: the ruler's arms, another Knight's, or the Domain's picture cut to a shield. A Domain with nothing painted flies nothing.

- Everyone sees the shields. A Holding you've hidden from the players keeps its shield hidden from them too, and shows it to you faintly.
- The shield fades with the **Holdings and Seats** slider, and redraws by itself when a Holding is granted, a Knight repaints their arms, or the Domain's choice changes.
- Turn them off for one Realm with the **Rulers' arms on Holdings** tick in [Realm Appearance](Realm-Appearance-and-Skins#icons-and-hex-lines).

## Rule page

### Lordship

- **Ruler**: who rules, as text. Pass On and Seize by Force fill it for you.
- **Successor**: whom the ruler names to follow them. Left blank, its placeholder shows the ruling Knight's own named successor from their sheet, and that is who Pass On offers.
- **Holding**: which Holding on the Realm map this Domain rules. Until one is picked, a Holding with the Domain's exact name counts, and the blank option says so.

Under these, a line says how many wilderness hexes the Holding's rule reaches (those nearer it than any other Holding) and names any Landmarks in them you know of. A warning line appears for any Knight holding a post here (ruler, Council seat or place at Court) that their Rank isn't worthy of yet.

### Authority: Warbands

The block says how many Warbands the Holding can muster (3 for a Seat of Power, 2 otherwise) and how many it has raised.

- **Muster a Warband** opens a picker. Once Import PDF has filled the Beasts & Hirelings compendium, it lists the book's Warbands with their stats; otherwise, or as well, pick **A Warband of Your Own** and fill in its stats on its sheet. Then pick the Warband's origin, one of **Loyal Vassals**, **Knights Who Share a Cause** or **Mercenaries**. Press **Muster Them**. See [Bringing In Your Rulebook](Bringing-In-Your-Rulebook).
- The new Warband is made as an NPC beside the Domain in the Actors tab, its sheet opens, and a card goes to chat. A Referee mustering it gives it the Domain's owners.
- Mustering past the limit asks first rather than refusing, since the Referee may allow it.
- Each raised Warband gets a row with its picture, name, SPI and origin. **Click the picture or the name** to open its sheet. A Warband that is Broken, Routed, Surrendered or Wiped Out is greyed and says so.
- **The walking-figure icon** on a row disbands it, after a confirmation. Their sheet stays in the Actors tab; they just stop being this Domain's.

**Upkeep** lives on the Warband's own sheet, not the Domain's: a Warband-scale NPC has an **Upkeep** button in its toolbar. Pick what wore them down (**Ill-rested**, **Poorly fed** or **Driven too hard**) and press **Roll SPI Loss** to take d6 SPI. The card says when they reach SPI 0 and stop taking orders. See [NPCs, Structures and Compendiums](NPCs-Structures-and-Compendiums).

### The Council

The Council has five seats: **Steward**, **Marshal**, **Sheriff**, **Envoy** and **The Circle**. Hover a seat's name for what it does (from your imported rulebook).

- **The four great seats** each have a drop-down listing the Court's Retainers who don't already hold a seat. You can't type a name in: take somebody into the Court as a Retainer first, then grant them the seat here. With no Retainer free, the list reads **No Retainers Available**.
- **The Circle** holds Knights, not Retainers. Its Knights' names are links to their sheets. **Click the person-plus icon** to tick which of the world's Knights (other than the ruler) sit in it, then **Seat Them**.
- Any of the four great seats left empty gets a warning under the Council heading, and each Season card lists Domains with empty seats.

### Council tasks

**Click a seat's name** (Steward, Marshal and so on) to set it a task. The name turns red under the pointer; on a sheet you can't edit it is a plain label. The **Council Task** window asks:

| Field | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **The task** | What they are to do, in your words. |
| **Scope** | How long the work takes: **A Phase**, then **A Week**, or else **A full Season**. |
| **Settled by** | **Nothing at risk: no roll**, **Luck Roll** (the default), or a Save in one Virtue. |

A Save is offered only when the seat's holder has a sheet to roll it with: a Circle of exactly one Knight, or a Retainer whose name matches an actor in the world. Press **Set the Task**, and a short card says who took it up.

The task appears indented under its seat, with its scope icon and a line giving its scope, what settles it and when it is due. A Phase's task is due at the next Phase, a Week's at the next Day (which is what **Weeks Pass** moves the calendar to), and a Season's at the next Season. A task that is due turns red, and the Phase and Season cards in chat list every Domain with tasks due.

- **The d6 icon** settles the task, due or not. A Luck Roll of 1 brings a Crisis, 2 to 3 means the work is done but something bad may come of it, and 4 to 6 means it is done. A passed Save is done and a failed one brings a Crisis. With nothing at risk it is simply done.
- A Crisis brought this way is rolled and added to the Domain for you. If the Domain already faces every Crisis, the card says the Referee decides what the failure brings instead.
- A Save task whose holder has since lost their sheet, been renamed, or been joined by more Knights in the Circle, falls back to a Luck Roll, and the card says why.
- **The × icon** sets the task aside unsettled, after a confirmation if anything was written.

Settling posts a **Council Task** card: who did it, what it was, the scope, the roll or Save, the outcome, and any Crisis with how to resolve it. The task leaves the sheet, and the card and a row on the Domain's Timeline thread are the record of it.

### Seize by Force

**Seize by Force** sits apart at the foot of the Rule page, to the right of **Pass On**. It asks who takes the Holding (one of the world's Knights from a list, or any name written in) and makes them ruler. The named successor is cleared, a card goes to chat, and the Rule page shows a turmoil notice until the Season turns. When it does, the Season card says the Domain has settled under its new ruler.

## Crises

The **Crises** block on the Court and Crises page lists each Crisis the Domain faces, with the book's lines on it and how it is resolved once the rulebook is imported. **Click the tick** on a row to resolve it; a short card says so.

- **Crisis Roll** rolls a d6: on a 1 two Crises arrive at once, on 2 to 3 two are drawn and a window asks which one the Domain takes (**Take this Crisis**), and on 4 to 6 none. Each Crisis is drawn on its own d6, and a Crisis the Domain already has passes to the next one down the list, so a roll always brings a new one while any are left. If you close the choosing window, the card names both and you add the chosen one with **Add Crisis** later.
- **Add Crisis** offers only the Crises the Domain doesn't already face.
- Under the list, a line says whether this Season's Crisis Roll has been made.
- With three or more Crises a notice warns that ending the Season like this brings misrule.

### Misrule

The **Misrule** tick box marks a Domain in misrule. It is ticked for you when a Season ends with three or more Crises unresolved, and the Season card says so. If the Domain has Knights in its Circle, the Circle window then opens so you can untick those who ride away. Resolving the Crisis that takes the count from three to two clears Misrule by itself; otherwise untick it by hand.

### When the Crisis Roll comes round

- **Each Season.** The Season card lists every Domain still owed its Crisis Roll and its Drama in Court. The Referee Toolkit's Time page lists them under **Crisis Rolls Due** with a **Crisis Roll** button each, and so does the [Ending a Session](Ending-a-Session) window. See [Time, Phases and Seasons](Time-Phases-and-Seasons).
- **Coming home from a long absence.** For a Domain ruled by a player's Knight, **Weeks Pass** or a Season turn while the Company is away from the Holding ticks **Away long**. When the Company next moves onto the Holding's hex, the Referee gets **Home from a Long Absence** with **Crisis Roll** or **Not now**. Any Crisis Roll clears the tick, and you can tick or untick it by hand. This needs the Domain's **Holding** set (or a Holding of the same name on the map).

### Increased Collections

A d6: on a 1 the Domain falls into misrule at once (Misrule is ticked), on 2 to 3 the coffers fill but a Crisis is drawn and added, and on 4 to 6 the Vassals pay.

## The Court

The **Court** block lists everyone serving the ruler, grouped as **Retainer**, **Courtier**, **Petitioner** and **Seer**. Each is one line: the name, the role, and a seat if they hold one (in bold) or serve one. Their note and leverage show underneath in grey when written.

- **Add to the Court** opens **Take Into the Court**: **Role**, **Name**, **Serves** (Retainers only: the Council member who took them on), **Leverage** (their hold over the ruler) and **Note**. The note's prompt changes with the role. A name is required.
- **The die beside Name** rolls a name from the ones the book gives on its Knights' pages, never one already in this Court while any are left. It needs the rulebook imported.
- **Click a member's name** to edit them in the same window. Making a seated Retainer anything else gives up their seat.
- **The person-× icon** lets them leave the Court, after a confirmation. A seat they held stands empty.

### Drama in Court

**Drama in Court** rolls a d6 for how the ruler is drawn in: on a 1 directly, on 2 to 3 through someone close, on 4 to 6 not at all. It picks a named Courtier at random to be at the heart of it (anyone named in the Court if there are no Courtiers) and shows their role, leverage and note. With the rulebook imported, it also rolls a prompt from the Drama Spark Table. The roll marks this Season's drama done, so the Season card stops listing it.

## Grand Designs

The **Grand Designs** block holds works the Domain has in hand. Begin one with a button for its size:

| Button | Done by |
| --- | --- |
| **Works** | The next Season. |
| **New building** | The next Season if begun on a Season's first Morning, otherwise the Season after. |
| **Grand project** | The next Age if begun on the first Morning of a Spring, otherwise the Age after. |

Type what is being built into the row. Each row says when it will be ready, then **Done**, with its edge turned red, once its time has come; each Season card lists the works that are done. **The × icon** strikes it off. A work struck off in the same Season it was begun, before it was done, is taken off the Timeline too, as a likely slip.

## Succession

- **Pass On** asks who succeeds the ruler. The named successor is filled in to start with, and the current ruler is left off the list of Knights. Pick a Knight or write a name, and press **Pass On**. The successor field is cleared, and the card reminds you the new ruler meets resistance.
- **A ruling Knight who is Slain** sends the Referee a whispered card naming the Domain and its successor, with a **Succession** button that opens Pass On.
- When the new ruler is one of the world's Knights, the Domain becomes theirs: their **Domain** button opens it, and the old ruler's button no longer does.

What happens to the player whose Knight died is on [Scars, Death and Successors](Scars-Death-and-Successors).

## The Knight sheet's Domain button

- The button reads the Domain's name and opens its sheet. While the Knight rules nothing it reads **Domain**, dimmed, and offers to found one.
- A player who doesn't own the Knight sees the button only when the Knight already rules a Domain.
- Squires never get the button.

> **Did you know?**
> - The Domain sheet's seat names are buttons: clicking **Steward** sets the Steward a task. There is no separate "add task" button.
> - Writing a Knight's name in **Ruler** (case doesn't matter) is enough for the header to show their arms, even without linking the Knight to the Domain.
> - A Domain's Warbands and Circle Knights are links: click a Warband's picture or a Circle Knight's name to open that sheet.
> - Pass On and Seize by Force write each new ruler onto the Domain's Timeline thread, and so do founding, Crisis Rolls, Drama, Court arrivals and departures, Circle changes, Warbands mustered and disbanded, Grand Designs begun and Council tasks settled.
