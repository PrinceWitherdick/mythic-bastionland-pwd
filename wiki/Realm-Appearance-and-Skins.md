# Realm Appearance and Skins

How a Realm is drawn is up to you: a **skin** (the pictures for terrain, rivers, Holdings, Landmarks and Myths) and a set of **colours** (the paper, ink, hex lines and Barriers). Each Realm Scene keeps its own look, and changing it never changes the Realm itself. You can also make a skin of your own pictures.

## Opening Realm Appearance

Any of these opens the **Realm Appearance** window (Referees only):

- **Realm Appearance** in the Realm tools (the crown in the scene controls).
- **Appearance** in the title bar of the Realm palette.
- **Choose the look** in Foundry's Configure Settings, or on the Referee Toolkit's Settings page.

A line at the top says which Realm you are changing: the Realm Scene in view, or, with no Realm on screen, the look new Realms start with.

![The Realm Appearance window, with the Skin cards (Blank Realm, Roundels, Woodcut, Surveyor's Atlas, Wax Seal, Armorial and Your own pictures), the Colours row, the Icons and Hex Lines sliders and the Preview](images/realm-appearance-and-skins-window.webp)

## Trying a look

- **Click a skin card** and **click a set of colours**. The Realm on screen is redrawn in them at once, for you only, and the **Preview** shows each picture of that skin.
- **Close the window** to put everything back as it was.
- **Apply** redraws that Realm Scene for everyone, in the new pictures, with its paper, hex lines and Barriers in the new colours, then closes the window. Other Realms keep their own look.

**Apply** stays greyed until something has changed. New Realms start with the look you last applied.

## Icons and Hex Lines

For a Realm laid over a map of your own that already draws its Holdings and Landmarks, such as a Hex Kit map, the system's icons can be faded so they don't sit on top of yours. This section shows only while a Realm Scene is in view.

- One slider each for **Holdings and Seats**, **Landmarks**, **Myths** and **Hex lines**.
- **Let go of a slider** to see it on the map, for you only. **Apply** saves it for everyone.
- Each slider sets every icon of its kind, even one you had faded by hand.
- A Landmark or Myth not yet found shows to you at half the strength you set.

The fading belongs to that Realm alone. New Realms start at full strength. See [Making a Realm](Making-a-Realm#importing-a-map-you-drew) for laying your own map under the hexes.

## The skins

| Skin | How it looks |
| --- | --- |
| **Blank Realm** | The free Blank Realm sheet's own inked terrain, Holdings and Landmarks, with rivers as the Realm Sheets draw them. The default. |
| **Roundels** | Pale hexes, fine marks and lettered roundels. |
| **Woodcut** | Bold lines, shields for Holdings and Myths, signs for Landmarks. |
| **Surveyor's Atlas** | Soft washes, fine marks and silhouettes. |
| **Wax Seal** | Strong colours and sealing-wax badges. |
| **Armorial** | Charges from old heraldry books: castles, crowns and beasts drawn in ink, Myths on shields. |

Two things only the **Blank Realm** skin does:

- **Several pictures to a terrain.** Most terrains have several drawings traced from the example map in the free Realm Sheets, picked per hex, with neighbours that stood side by side on the example laid side by side again. Click a hex again with the same terrain brush to step it to its next picture. See [Painting and Editing the Realm](Painting-and-Editing-the-Realm#terrain-pictures).
- **Joined lakes.** Lakes side by side are drawn as one water, with a shore where they meet land and a mouth where a river runs in.

## The colours

**Parchment** (the default), **Ink on White**, **Verdigris & Brass**, **Azure & Gules**, **Ochre & Oxblood**, **Ash & Iron** and **Candlelit Night**. Every skin comes in every set.

## A Skin of Your Own

The **Your own pictures** card opens **A Skin of Your Own**, for making a skin from pictures you supply. A saved skin can be chosen in every world on this Foundry server.

1. Give it a **Name**.
2. Under **Pictures you don't give**, choose the shipped skin that fills in whatever you leave out.
3. Under **Your terrain**, choose whether your terrain pictures fill their hex or sit inside it.
4. **Add pictures** from your computer, or **Take them from a folder** already on the server. Name each file after the picture it replaces, such as `forest.png` or `holding-castle.webp`. The `terrain-`, `holding-` and `landmark-` part can be left off, so `marsh`, `tower` or `ruin` work too. Any image type does.
5. **File names to use** folds out the full list, with a tick beside each name you have given. A count says how many of the Realm's pictures you have given; files whose names match none are left out, and you're told how many.
6. **Save the skin.**

**Start again** clears the pictures you have added.

Your skin appears among the others in Realm Appearance, tagged **Yours**. Its card has a **pencil** to change it and a **bin** to take it off the list in every world. Realms already drawn with it keep their pictures.

Your skins and their pictures are kept in `mythic-bastionland-art/realm-skins/` in your Foundry data folder, outside any world, which is why every world sees them.

## The Seat of Power and the Holdings

- The Seat of Power wears a heraldic crown in every skin.
- On the Roundels, Woodcut, Surveyor's Atlas and Wax Seal skins, the town, castle, tower and fortress are icons from game-icons.net. The Blank Realm skin's Holdings are traced from the sheet, and the Armorial skin's from heraldry.
