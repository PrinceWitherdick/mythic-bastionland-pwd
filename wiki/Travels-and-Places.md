# Travels and Places

Your Company keeps a record of where it has been on each Realm: every hex it has visited, what was found there, what the Referee told you about it, and the notes you keep together. You read it in the **Places** window and on the **Travels** tab of your Knight's sheet. It never shows anything your Company hasn't found.

This page is the players' side. The Referee's side of the same window is on [Places and the Lay of the Land](Places-and-the-Lay-of-the-Land).

## What you can see

A hex is one of your places once the Company has:

- been there,
- been told about it by the Referee,
- seen it from afar, from a vantage point,
- or written a note on it.

Only those hexes open in Places, and only those show a name the Referee has given them. Every Holding you can see on the map is drawn on the chart, even before you visit it. Myths, Landmarks and Barriers stay off your record until they are revealed or found.

## Opening Places

- **Places**, the map-and-pin button just left of the sidebar on a Realm.
- **Places** on your hotbar (slot 2).
- **Double-click open ground** on the Realm with the Token tools in hand. A hex you don't know yet only tells you so.
- **Double-click the Company Token** to open the hex it stands in.
- **Click a row** on your Knight's **Travels** tab. See [below](#the-travels-tab).

![The Places window as a player sees it, the Chart on the left, and on the right the hex with What the Referee Told You, two told notes with their dates, and The Company's Note](images/travels-and-places-player-window.webp)

## The three pages

| Page | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Places** | Your places in three lists: **Places of Note** (where you found something, were told something or wrote a note), **Wilderness**, and **Heard Of** (not reached yet). |
| **Chart** | A drawn chart of the Realm as your Company knows it, with a key. |
| **Journey** | Where the Company went, Season by Season, each line with its Phase: arrivals, tellings, Barriers met and notes written. |

- **Search the places** narrows all three pages.
- **Show only** narrows the Places list: **All**, **Holdings**, **Landmarks**, **Myths**, **Told**, **Noted** or **Not Visited**.
- **Order** sorts it by **Last reached first**, **By name** or **Most visited first**.
- **Hover a row** to ring that hex on the map. The **crosshair** at a row's end shows the hex on the map, ringed in green until your next click.
- **Click a row, a hex on the chart, or a line of the Journey** to open that hex on the right.

The page you last chose is remembered in your browser.

### Zooming the chart

- **Ctrl and the mouse wheel** zoom about the pointer. A plain wheel scrolls past the chart, as it scrolls anything else.
- **Double-click** the chart to zoom in there. Double-click again to see it whole.
- Once zoomed, **drag with either mouse button** to move about.
- **Whole chart** zooms back out.
- **Show the way the Company went** draws your route.

## A hex in your Places

The right side shows the hex you chose:

- **Its name**, if the Referee gave it one, with its column and row underneath.
- What stands there that you have found, and **The Company is here** if you are.
- How often and when the Company came.
- A line for anything **seen from afar** there, and for each Barrier that turned one of you back.
- **What the Referee Told You**: each thing the Referee has told the Company about this hex, with when.
- **The Company's Note**.

**Ping**, in the window's title bar, shows the hex on the map ringed in green until your next click.

### The Company's Note

One note per hex, shared by the whole table. Anyone can write it: type in the box, and it is kept when you leave it. A line under it says who last wrote it, and when.

The note is kept through the Referee's browser, so a Referee has to be signed in. Without one, the box says it can be written once a Referee is here, and what you typed stays in the box.

## The Travels tab

![The Knight sheet's Travels page open on the Chart, the places the Company has been drawn on a small hex map below the filter pills](images/travels-and-places-travels-tab.webp)

Every Knight's sheet, and every Squire's, has a **Travels** tab with the same three pages for the Realm. Clicking a place there opens Places on that hex. On the sheet the chart is kept to a comfortable size and centred, so widening the sheet doesn't grow it.

## Visited marks on the map

Every hex the Company has come into is marked on the map. Each person chooses for themselves:

- **The footprints** button, just left of the sidebar above Places, shows or hides the marks in your browser only.
- **Right-click the footprints** to open **Visited Marks** and choose the mark: a **Pencilled Ring**, a **Tick** (the default), a **Cross**, or the **Hex Edge** drawn over in a strong line. Each has a colour of its own to pick. **Default Colours** puts them back. Changes show at once.

The same window opens from your sheet's Settings page and from Foundry's own settings. See [Your Settings and Accessibility](Your-Settings-and-Accessibility).

## The Company Token

One Token stands for the whole Company on the Realm. It belongs to no Actor, so every player can move it.

- **Drag it** to travel. A Barrier or the edge of the map refuses the move. Running into a hidden Barrier shows it on the map for everyone, and the Referee is told.
- **Double-click it** to open its hex in Places.
- As you move, the Referee is told what each hex calls for: a Myth's Omen, a Holding's mood, a Gallop. See [Exploring the Wilderness](Exploring-the-Wilderness#when-the-company-moves).

### Its picture

The Referee chooses the Token's picture when the Realm is made, and can change it later: **right-click the Token** and press the **flag** in its HUD, or use **Company picture** on the Referee Toolkit's Settings page. The window offers eight icons, a colour for them (ink, the heraldic tinctures, or a colour of your own), or any picture of the Referee's own. A picture of the Referee's own is kept in the gallery to choose again; the **×** in its corner takes it out of the gallery again.

### If the Token goes missing

If the Company Token is deleted, the Referees are whispered **The Company has left the map**, with **Put them back** to stand them where they were. Whenever a Realm has no Company Token, a **Place the Company** button stands over the top edge of the map for the Referee: click it, then click the hex they stand in (right-click or **Escape** to stop).

## Asking the Referee

The Travel and Rest and Exploration panels beside the map hold the travel rules. Where a rule is something the Company can choose to do, a player gets an **Ask the Referee** button: a Gallop, a Wilderness Roll, a search, a look from a vantage point, or asking somebody for folklore. Pressing it whispers the Referee a card naming who asked, with a button that does it.

To hide the panels in your browser only, untick **Travel Rules beside the Realm Map** in your settings.

## The hex under the pointer

Point at a hex of the Realm and a label under the map names what you know of it: its terrain, what stands there, whether the Company has been there, what you were told and your note. Untick **Name the Realm Hex under the Pointer** in your settings to hide it.
