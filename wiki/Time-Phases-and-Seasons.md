# Time, Phases and Seasons

The system keeps one calendar for the world: the **Phase** of the day (Morning, Afternoon, Night), the **Season** (Spring, Harvest, Winter) and the **Age**. Everyone sees it on a bar at the top of the screen. The Referee moves it on, and each step carries out what the book says happens between Phases, Seasons and Ages: the Wilderness Roll and the Night's hardships, the Season's events, Virtues restored, pursuits, Glory, old age, and the Domains' Crisis Rolls. This page is mostly for Referees; players just watch the bar and the chat cards.

The book never numbers a Season's days, so neither does the system. A Season is marked by its events, not by dates.

## The bar at the top of the screen

A bar at the top centre of the screen shows the **Phase**, the **Season** and the **Age** (as "2nd Age"), each in its own colour: bright for Morning, amber for Afternoon, dark for Night, green for Spring, gold for Harvest, pale blue for Winter. The Phase and Season colours match the chat cards their changes post. When one changes, its part slides over to the new face on every screen at the table.

- **Hover the bar** to read the full date, such as "Night, in the Winter of the 2nd Age."
- **Hide it in your own browser** with **Phase, Season and Age at the Top of the Screen**, in the Windows group of the Settings page on your Knight sheet or the Referee Toolkit. Everyone else keeps theirs.
- **Reduce Motion** stops the slide.

For the Referee, each part of the bar is a button:

- **Click the Phase, Season or Age** to let down the time menu under the bar: **Weeks Pass**, **Months Pass**, **A Year Passes**, and **End the Session** under them.
- **Hover Weeks Pass** to see which seasonal event it carries the Company on to.
- **Click anywhere else, or press Escape,** to put the menu away.

There is no Next Phase on the bar. That button is on the Referee Toolkit's **Time** page, below.

## The Time page

The Referee Toolkit's **Time** tab (the hourglass on its rail) is where time is moved on and the Season is kept. You can also open it from the **Time** button at the top of the Roll Tables sidebar. See [The Referee Toolkit](The-Referee-Toolkit) for the rest of the Toolkit.

From the top, the page holds:

| Section | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **This Season: Spring** (or the Season you are in) | The Season's three events, a **Weeks Pass** button, and **Your notes on this Season**. |
| **Move Time On** | **Next Phase**, **Months Pass**, **A Year Passes**, **Journey to a Distant Realm** and **End the Session**. |
| **Off course** | Shown only while a Curse's blight still follows the Company. See [Myths and Omens](Myths-and-Omens). |
| **Crisis Rolls Due** | Every Domain that hasn't made this Season's Crisis Roll, each with a **Crisis Roll** button. |
| **Myths to Replace** | Resolved Myths waiting for a new one. See [Myths and Omens](Myths-and-Omens). |
| **Glory** | **Myth Resolved**, **Tournament Won** and **Battle Won**. |
| **Past Seasons** | A record of every earlier Season, grouped by Age. |

## Next Phase and the Phase's end window

**Click Next Phase** on the Time page to end the Phase. Before the calendar moves, one window asks everything about the Phase that is ending. Its title names it: **The Morning Ends**, **The Afternoon Ends**, **The Night Ends**. Only the parts that apply are shown:

- **Where the Company stands**, read from the Company's Token on the Realm map.
- **How did the Company spend the Phase?** Pick **Travelling or exploring**, **Camping outdoors** or **Indoors, as guests**. The window guesses for you: indoors in a Holding, camping for a Night spent still, travelling otherwise. Hover each choice for what it costs.
- **Wilderness Roll**: a **Make the Wilderness Roll** tick, ticked when the hex calls for one. In a Myth's hex it offers that Myth's next Omen instead. In a Holding there is no roll. Spending the Phase indoors cancels the roll whatever is ticked.
- **As Morning Comes** (only as the Night ends): a row for every Knight, ticked if a player owns them, with **No sleep** and **Deprived** ticks. The rules above the rows say what the Night already costs for the way it was spent, for Winter, and for dire weather. Tick **No sleep** for anyone else kept from proper sleep, and **Deprived** for anyone without food, water or fire. Last Morning's Deprived ticks come back ticked, so you only change what changed.
- **Breaking Camp** (only as the Night ends, once your rulebook is imported): **Roll the day's sky and weather**, ticked.
- **Dying Untended**: anyone still Mortally Wounded, ticked, since nobody survives a Phase of that untended. **Untick anyone who was cared for.**

**Click Move On to the Morning** (or Afternoon, or Night) to carry it out. Closing the window instead leaves the Phase where it was.

What follows, in order:

1. The Wilderness Roll, if ticked, made while the old Phase still stands. If the Company camped and the roll showed an Omen, a second window asks whether it was hostile; a hostile one costs everybody their sleep.
2. The calendar moves on and the bar turns.
3. Anyone left dying untended dies, on one card.
4. A Phase card goes to chat in the new Phase's colours. As Morning comes, it carries **What the Night Cost**: every Virtue each Knight lost, rolled and taken. It also lists Council tasks that have come due and Knights' tables that come round again.
5. The day's sky and weather are rolled and whispered to the Referee, and the map's weather follows (see [Weather](#weather)).
6. Dire Weather is rolled, where the land is at its mercy.

The window doesn't open at all when no Realm shows the Company, no Night is ending and nobody is dying; the Phase just moves on. Only one Phase can end at a time: a second Next Phase while the window is open just says so.

![The Night Ends window, with Camping outdoors picked, the Make the Wilderness Roll tick, the As Morning Comes rows for the Company with No sleep and Deprived boxes, and Roll the day's sky and weather](images/time-phases-and-seasons-night-ends.webp)

> **Did you know?**
> - Other things that take a whole Phase open the same window: wasting a Phase at a Barrier (its button reads **Waste the Morning**), a search that takes the Phase, and a Remedy. See [Exploring the Wilderness](Exploring-the-Wilderness).
> - The Night's travel question (a guide and light, or travelling blind) is asked when the Company first moves by night. What the Night costs is taken only as it ends, in this window.

## Weeks Pass and the Season's events

Each Season begins with a Feast, has a mass in its middle and ends with the Realm's collection (the Tax, the Tithe or the Levy). The **This Season** section lists all three in order, with where each falls (such as "Spring begins" or "Midspring"). The line under each event comes from your imported rulebook.

- **Click Came to pass** beside an event to mark it, without moving time. Its card goes to chat in the Season's colours.
- **Click Weeks Pass** (on the Time page or the bar) to carry the Company on to the next event. That event is marked, its card is posted, and the calendar moves to a new Morning. No Phase's end window opens.
- When only the collection is left, it reads **Ends the Season**, and Weeks Pass turns the Season instead.

The collection's card names the Domains that gather it. The Season's events are the Referee's only; players don't see the marks.

## Months Pass: turning the Season

**Click Months Pass** (on the Time page or the bar). A window opens listing, under **Who takes part**, every Knight: ticked when a player owns them or their Token is selected. Each ticked Knight picks a pursuit (Pilgrimage, Courtesy or Service). Service and Courtesy then offer a second menu for what came of it, or **Ask once the Season turns**. Untick anyone who isn't part of it.

**Click Months Pass** in that window. Then:

- The calendar moves to the first Morning of the next Season. After Winter comes the next Spring of the same Age.
- Each ticked Knight has their Virtues restored, and any Scar that settles with the Season is settled. A Knight whose Doom lifts is told so.
- Everyone else who is short of Virtues is restored too, NPCs and unticked Knights alike. The dead and the dying are left as they are.
- The Season's collection is marked as come to pass, and every Domain is settled. A Domain that falls into misrule is asked which of its Circle ride away. See [Domains and Courts](Domains-and-Courts).
- A Season card goes to chat in the new Season's colours: each Knight on one line, then what is now due: Crisis Rolls, Drama in Court, Myths to replace (with a button for each), empty Council seats, finished Grand Designs, Council tasks and Knights' tables.
- The turn is written into **Past Seasons** and onto [The Campaign Timeline](The-Campaign-Timeline).

## A Year Passes: turning the Age

**Click A Year Passes** (on the Time page or the bar). The same kind of window opens, with the Age's pursuits (Duty, Succession, Legacy). A Knight with a stage of life to grow into also has a **Grows Mature** or **Grows Old** tick, ticked to start with.

On top of everything a Season's turn does:

- The new Age begins in Spring.
- Each ticked Knight gains 1 Glory.
- The Old lose d12 VIG from their maximum. Anyone it takes to VIG 0 dies peacefully.
- **Succession** asks the Knight to name a successor, and offers to Knight one who is still a Squire. **Legacy** gives the successor half the Knight's Glory, asking for a successor if none is named. See [Scars, Death and Successors](Scars-Death-and-Successors).
- NPCs and Referee-played Knights who are Old lose their VIG too, on one card.
- Knights ticked to grow older reroll their Virtues for it. Then a second window offers every NPC with an Age, unticked, so you choose who else grows older.

## Journey to a Distant Realm

**Click Journey to a Distant Realm** on the Time page. If the world has other Realm Scenes, pick one, or **Somewhere Else**. The Season turns as it does for Months Pass, but nobody picks a pursuit, since the Company is on the road. The card says the Company arrived. If you picked one of your Realm Scenes, it becomes the active Scene.

## Setting the calendar by hand

The Toolkit's banner holds the calendar at its right end: the **Season** over the **Age**, then the **Phase** with the weather under it. Use these to fix a mistake or start a game partway through, not to play out time.

- **Pick a Season or Phase from its drop-down** to set it by hand. A new Phase still posts its Phase card, but no Phase's end window opens, nothing is rolled and nobody loses anything. A Season set by hand turns nothing and records nothing in Past Seasons.
- **Click the Age number** to open **The Age**. **A Year Passes** turns the Age properly; **Set the Age** (the default) only changes the number.
- **Hover the Age** to read the full date.

## Crisis Rolls due and long absences

Every Domain makes its Crisis Roll as a Season begins. The Time page's **Crisis Rolls Due** lists each Domain that hasn't rolled this Season, with its ruling Knight's name and a **Crisis Roll** button. The same list appears in [Ending a Session](Ending-a-Session) as **Crisis Rolls still owed**.

A ruler coming home after a long time away also owes one. The system counts an absence as long when Weeks Pass or a Season's turn happens while the Company is away from the Domain's Holding, for a Domain ruled by a player's Knight. When the Company's Token next enters that Holding, a **Home from a Long Absence** window offers the **Crisis Roll** or **Not now**. The Domain sheet's Holding picker and "Away long" tick, and the roll itself, are covered in [Domains and Courts](Domains-and-Courts).

## Glory

The **Glory** section on the Time page has a button for each way of winning Glory other than a new Age: **Myth Resolved**, **Tournament Won** and **Battle Won**. Hover one for when it applies.

- **Click a Glory button**, tick every Knight who earns it, and **click Award Glory**. Each gains 1 Glory, and a card names them with their new Glory and any new rank. The award also goes on the Timeline.
- A new Age gives each Knight who takes part 1 Glory by itself.
- End the Session's window has the same three buttons.

## Past Seasons

Every Season that has ended, that you wrote notes on, or that saw a Myth completed is kept in **Past Seasons**, with the newest Age first. The Age you are in starts open; older Ages are folded. Each Season folds to one row naming how it ended ("Harvest Begins", "Age 3 Begins", "Arrived in Winter").

Open a Season to see:

- the Myths completed in it, or "No Myth was completed this Season.",
- **Your notes on this Season**, still editable,
- what its card told the table as it ended, one line per Knight.

Notes typed in **This Season** stay with that Season once it turns. Past Seasons are for the Referee's eyes only.

## Weather

### The weather button

With the FXMaster (or FXMaster+) module on, the Toolkit's banner has a **weather** button under the Phase. **Click it** to open **Weather** and pick one of ten skies: Clear, Fair, Overcast, Windy, Fog, Rain, Downpour, Storm, Snow, Blizzard. FXMaster draws it on the Scene the players are on, and it follows them when you change Scenes. Your own FXMaster effects are left alone.

- **Take It Off the Map** (in the same window) keeps the weather set but clears it from every Scene. The banner's weather is struck through while it's off. **Put It Back on the Map** restores it.
- Each morning, ticking **Roll the day's sky and weather** in The Night Ends rolls the Nature Spark Tables' Sky and Weather and whispers the result to the Referee. The map takes on the rolled weather, and the card says "The map now shows: ..." Heavy rain draws as a downpour. The roll never draws snow or a blizzard; pick those yourself.
- Fog on that roll can hide the way. Solid fog comes down at once; other fog gives the card a **Let the Fog Hide the Way** button, and solid fog a **The Fog Lifts** button. See [Exploring the Wilderness](Exploring-the-Wilderness).

Without FXMaster, no button is shown. A Referee whose world doesn't have it gets a one-time chat card saying what it adds.

### Dire Weather

The world setting **Dire Weather** says when the land suffers it: **Never**, **In Winter, as in most Realms** (the default) or **All year round**. While it applies, the Dire Weather table is rolled each time Next Phase begins a new Phase, unless the Company is in a Holding or isn't on a Realm. Two looming results in a row count as dire. A dire result rolled during a Night means nobody outdoors gets proper sleep, which The Night Ends lists in its rules.

You can also roll it by hand: **Roll Dire Weather** in the travel rules beside the map, or **Referee Rolls** at the top of the Roll Tables sidebar.

### Weather settings

| Setting | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Show the Weather Button** | Settings page, Weather on the Map | Hides the banner's weather button in your browser. The weather last set still follows the table. |
| **Weather on the Map** | Settings page, Weather on the Map | The same as Take It Off the Map, for the whole world. |
| **Types of Weather** | Settings page, Weather on the Map | Ticks for Clouds, Fog, Rain, Hail, Snow, Blizzard Snow and Storm Light. Untick one to keep that part off the map. |
| **Dire Weather** | Settings page, For the Referee | When the Dire Weather table is rolled. |
| **Phase, Season and Age at the Top of the Screen** | Settings page, Windows | Shows or hides the bar, per browser. |

All of these are also in Foundry's own settings. See [World Settings Reference](World-Settings-Reference).
