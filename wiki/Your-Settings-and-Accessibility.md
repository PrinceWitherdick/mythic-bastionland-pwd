# Your Settings and Accessibility

Each person at the table can change how the system's sheets, windows and chat cards look for them: text size, colour scheme, contrast, typeface, italics and movement. These are your own settings, kept in your browser, and nobody else at the table sees the difference. This page also covers the Referee's part of the same tab.

## Where to find them

- **The Settings tab on your Knight.** It is the sliders icon on the sheet's tab rail. It shows only on a Knight that is yours: one you play as, or, for a player, one you own. Another player's Knight never shows it.
- **The Settings tab on the Referee Toolkit**, for the Referee. It has the same settings, plus groups for the Referee. See [The Referee Toolkit](The-Referee-Toolkit).
- **Foundry's Configure Settings window**, under the Mythic Bastionland section. It holds the same settings, so a change in one place shows in the other at once.

Changes take effect straight away; nothing needs a reload except the Campaign Timeline switch. **All Settings…** at the foot of the tab opens Foundry's Configure Settings window on the system's section, for anything not on the tab.

## Reading

| Setting | Choices | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Text Size** | 0.90 to 1.40 (default 1.00) | The size of the writing on the system's sheets, windows and chat cards, and of the pictures beside it. Drag the slider; the number follows it, and the size is saved when you let go. |
| **Colour Scheme** | Parchment (default), Lamplit, Midnight, Ashen, Follow Foundry | The paper and ink. See [Colour schemes](#colour-schemes). |
| **Contrast** | Normal (default), High Contrast | High Contrast sharpens whichever scheme you read in, and makes faint and coloured writing clearer. Pair it with a larger Text Size. |
| **Typeface** | Old Print, as in the Book (default); Plain Serif; Signika, as in Foundry's Own Windows | The lettering. The two plainer faces also set the blackletter titles in small capitals. |
| **No Italic Text** | Off (default) | Sets every slanted word upright, everywhere in Foundry, not only on the system's sheets. Italics that mark something out, such as the hint under a field, then read like the text around them. |
| **Reduce Motion** | Off (default) | Turns off the fades on the system's sheets and windows, and moves the map straight to a hex shown from the Referee Toolkit instead of panning there. Attack effects keep their sounds but lose their animation. |
| **Rule Word Popups** | On (default) | Hovering a bold rule word, such as Exposed, Hefty or Gambit, shows what the book says it means. Off, the words read as plain text. See [The Rulebook Reader](The-Rulebook-Reader#rule-word-popups). |

If your operating system asks for reduced motion, the system's animations follow that as well, even with **Reduce Motion** off.

### Colour schemes

| Scheme | Looks like |
|---|---|
| **Parchment** | The book's look: dark ink on parchment. |
| **Lamplit (Dark, Warm)** | Warm brown paper with light ink. |
| **Midnight (Dark, Blue)** | Deep blue paper. |
| **Ashen (Dark, Charcoal)** | Plain charcoal paper. |
| **Follow Foundry** | Parchment while Foundry's own windows are light, Lamplit while they are dark. It follows Foundry's interface setting as it changes, or your browser's light or dark preference while Foundry's is left at its default. |

The coloured cards for the Phase and the Season keep their own inks under a dark scheme. High Contrast works with every scheme: Parchment turns to plain white paper in black ink, a dark scheme to near-black paper in near-white ink.

## Windows

| Setting | Default | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Larger Art on Hover** | On | Shows a larger copy of a Knight's, Seer's or Myth's picture beside it while the pointer is over it. |
| **Reopen Sheets on Reload** | On | Reopens the Knight, item and journal sheets and the rulebook you had open, where you left them, when you reload Foundry. |
| **Phase, Season and Age at the Top of the Screen** | On | The bar at the top of the screen with the time of day, the Season and the Age. See [Time, Phases and Seasons](Time-Phases-and-Seasons). |
| **Travel Rules beside the Realm Map** | On | Travel on the left of a Realm's map and Rest and Exploration on its right. A Referee who hides them hides the roll buttons they hold too. See [Exploring the Wilderness](Exploring-the-Wilderness). |
| **Name the Realm Hex under the Pointer** | On | The label under the map naming the terrain of the hex you point at, and what stands there. |
| **Mark the Hexes the Company Has Been To** | On | The marks on every hex of a Realm the Company has come into. The footprints button beside the sidebar does the same. |
| **Start the Tours by Themselves** | On | Whether Foundry Tours about making a Realm start on their own. Off, they wait in **Tour Management** for you to play. |

Under them, **Choose the Mark** opens the **Visited Marks** window: pick a **Pencilled Ring**, a **Tick** (the default), a **Cross** or the **Hex Edge**, each in a colour of your choosing, with **Default Colours** to go back. **Right-clicking the footprints button** beside the sidebar opens the same window. See [Travels and Places](Travels-and-Places).

Every setting in these two groups changes only your browser. Hiding the Phase bar or the travel rules, for instance, hides them for you alone; everyone else keeps theirs.

## For the Referee

The Toolkit's Settings tab has two more groups, shown to the Referee alone. Most of their settings change the game for everyone, and each such row says **For the whole table.** Their full descriptions are in the [World Settings Reference](World-Settings-Reference).

**For the Referee**

- **Solo Play**, **Allow the Same Knight Twice**, **Reaching a New Hex** (yours alone), **Roll a New Hex's Land**, **Journal Entries for Hexes**, **Journal Entries for Sites**, **Show Hex Column and Row**, **Dire Weather**, **Let Players Open the Rulebook**, **Attack Effects on the Map** and **Campaign Timeline**. Changing **Campaign Timeline** asks to reload.
- **Choose the look** opens **Realm Appearance**. See [Realm Appearance and Skins](Realm-Appearance-and-Skins).
- **Open the Welcome** opens the Welcome window again. See [Bringing In Your Rulebook](Bringing-In-Your-Rulebook).
- **Company picture** changes the picture the Company's Token carries on the Realm, even before it is on the map.

**Weather on the Map** (these need FXMaster)

- **Show the Weather Button** (yours alone): the weather button in the Toolkit's banner.
- **Weather on the Map**: whether the weather is drawn on the players' Scene at all.
- **Types of Weather**: one drop-down of tick boxes for Clouds, Fog, Rain, Hail, Snow, Blizzard Snow and Storm Light. Shut, it reads how many are on, such as **All 7 on** or **5 of 7 on**. Hover a type to see what it draws. It shuts on a click elsewhere on the sheet, or on `Esc`.
