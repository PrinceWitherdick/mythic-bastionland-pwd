# Property, Steeds and Squires

The **Property, Steed and Squire** page of the Knight sheet holds everything a Knight owns: the steed they ride and the Squire who serves them on the left, and their weapons, armour and gear on the right with the Armour total above. The page's icon on the tab rail shows what carries all of it: a horse while the Knight rides a steed, the Squire's kneeling figure while one serves them, and a backpack otherwise.

A Knight chosen from the book arrives with their Property already filled in: each line of it read into a weapon, a piece of armour or gear, with dice, tags and Armour set, plus the standard kit every Knight carries. See [Choosing a Knight](Choosing-a-Knight).

## The Property list

Possessions keep a fixed order: weapons first, biggest die leading, then shields, then armour from the head outward (helm, coats with padding under mail, then plates), then everything else. You can drag a row up or down among things of its own kind; dropped among another kind it snaps back and says why.

Each row reads like a line in the book: the item's short name in bold, the rest of the line after it, then its tags in brackets (dice, Hefty, Long, Armour and so on).

- **Click an item's name** to show it in chat (its tooltip says **Show in chat**). This works even for someone who can only view the sheet.
- **Hover the row** to show its tools on the right. The pencil (**Edit**) opens the item's own window. There is no delete button on the row: delete from the item's window, after reading it.
- **The fist or shield at the left** marks a weapon wielded or a piece of armour worn (**Worn or wielded**). Putting on a second piece of one kind (a second helm, say) takes the first off, with a note saying so.
- **Minus and plus** appear on anything counted, such as javelins: **Use one** and **Add one**.
- A broken or used-up item is drawn faded until it is mended or restocked.

### Armour

The **A** badge beside the Property heading (and again beside VIG on the Knight page) is the Knight's Armour total. Hover it to see which pieces make it up.

- Armour that counts only sometimes (**When mounted**, **When Wounded**, **Only when...**, **Except when...**) follows the Mounted and Wounded condition pills by itself.
- For **Only when...** and **Except when...** armour, a toggle appears on the row while you hover it: switch it to say whether the situation holds right now. Its tooltip says whether the piece's Armour counts.
- A shield toggle drawn faded means the piece is worn but its Armour doesn't count right now.

### Buttons some rows carry

Hover a row to find these, where the item has them:

| Button | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flask | A Remedy | **Use Remedy**: restores its Virtue to everyone ticked, steeds, ponies and followers included, and uses it up. |
| Wine glass | A Passion | Indulge it to restore SPI. |
| Die | An item with odds | Roll its chance (such as 1 in 2) of holding what's wanted. |
| Broken heart | An item that costs a Virtue | Take the loss when something befalls it. |
| Die (d6) | An Ability with a die for the fight | During a Combat, add the die to every Attack until the fight is over. |
| Coins / Star | Some Abilities | Flip a coin for a life, or etch a rune at sunset. |

Nobody, person or pack beast, can carry two Remedies. Giving a second to someone who already carries one is refused, and the warning names a steed that could carry it instead.

## Adding things

- **Click + Weapon, + Armour or + Gear** under the list. The item's window opens over a new, unsaved item: fill it in and press **Save** to add it, or **Cancel** (or close the window) to add nothing.
- **Drag an item** from the Items tab, a compendium or another sheet onto the Knight's sheet to add a copy.

An item with no picture of its own gets a matching game-icons.net picture (a sword, a helm, a horse head and so on), which follows its name if you rename it. A picture you choose yourself is never replaced.

### The item window

The item's window holds everything the system reads from it: damage dice, Hefty, Long, Slow, Ranged, Blast, Ignores Armour, Trample, Specialist, Wooden, **Another way to fight** for a weapon used two ways, Armour and the **Counts** rule for armour, Rarity, Remedy, Poison, **Carried** and **Restocked** for things that are counted, **Broken**, and a Description. Hover a field's label for what it does. A saved item's window has **Delete** in its footer (it asks first).

Things marked **Restocked** each new Season or each day fill back up to their full stock, and are mended, when the calendar turns.

## The table on your Knight's page

Most Knights' pages in the book carry a d6 table tied to one of their possessions (the one that says "see below"), and a few have a table about the Knight themself. Once the rulebook is imported:

- **A d6 die** sits under that possession on the Property page (or on a row of its own at the foot of the list). **Click it** to open the table in its own window. What you have rolled shows after the die, as a sentence where the table has one.
- In the window, **click the die** or **Roll** to roll every column, **click a column heading** to roll just that one, or **click an entry** to take it by hand (click it again to let it go). Rolls are posted to chat. **Done** closes the window.
- When a result describes a possession (what form a weapon takes, say), it is written onto that possession. If more than one could take it, **Which Possession?** asks.
- Someone who can only view the Knight can still open the table to read what was rolled.

Some tables come round again, each new Season, each night or each day. For those the die sits inside the possession's brackets, right after the words saying when, and is marked **Due** once that time has come since the last roll. You are also told when the calendar turns.

## Steeds

A steed is an NPC of its own that the Knight rides: it has its own sheet, can be targeted in combat, and its Trample joins a mounted charge.

- **Click Take a Steed** in the empty Steed box. Pick one of the book's steeds (once the rulebook is imported), or **No stats yet** for one to fill in yourself, and give it a name or leave the box empty to name it later. The steed is made in the Knight's folder and its sheet opens.
- **Or drag an NPC** from the Actors tab onto the Knight's sheet to ride it. (Drag from the Actors tab, not a compendium, so it can be targeted.)
- **Click the steed's name** to open its sheet, the pencil beside it to **Name your steed**, or the × to stop riding it (the steed itself is kept).

Under the name the steed's kind is shown (when its name doesn't already say it) and its Trample. A steed you name keeps what the book called it as the line under its name on its own sheet, and its sheet says which Knight owns it.

Taking a steed makes an actor, so a player needs Foundry's Create Actors permission (granted to players by default on a new world; see [Choosing a Knight](Choosing-a-Knight#making-a-knight)).

A Knight whose book Property includes a steed or a beast companion (a hawk, a hound) gets those made as NPCs when they're chosen. Choosing a new Knight on the same sheet deletes those again; a steed you took yourself stays.

## Squires

A Knight in a Company of 2 Knights or fewer may keep a Squire (the book's p7).

- **Click Take a Squire** in the empty Squire box. The system rolls 2d6 for each Virtue, sets GD 1, rolls a d6 for one piece of extra equipment, and makes the Squire with a dagger, that equipment and a pony to ride, named "Squire to (your Knight)". The rolls go to chat and the Squire's sheet opens.
- **Or drag a Squire** from the Actors tab onto the Knight's sheet. If they serve another Knight, or this Knight already has a Squire, it asks first and unlinks the old ones. A Squire dropped by the Referee on a player's Knight goes to that player.
- **Click the Squire's name** to open their sheet, or the × to unlink them (the Squire is kept). Unlinking clears both sheets.
- A Slain Squire can't be Knighted.

The Company is counted as one Knight for each player who owns one, so a fallen Knight and their successor count once. In a bigger Company the empty Squire box says so, and **Take a Squire** is greyed out for players. The Referee can still grant one: they're asked to confirm. If a Company grows past 2 while Squires remain, the Referee gets a private note; whether the Squires stay is their call.

### The Squire sheet

A Squire is the same kind of actor as a Knight, drawn as the single page the book gives them: Virtues, Guard, Armour, Conditions, Recovery, Age, their own Property and steed, Scars and Gambits. They have no Glory, Rank, Feats, Seer, successor or Oath. Their header says **Squire to** and names the Knight they serve (click to open that Knight's sheet), and the action bar has Attack, Duel and Take Damage. Their tab rail is **Squire**, **Travels**, **Timeline**, **Chronicle** and, on your own, **Settings**.

### Knighting a Squire

- **Click Knight this Squire** in the Squire sheet's title bar and confirm. Each Virtue rises by d6 (posted to chat), Glory starts at 0, and they leave their Knight's service.
- **Choose a Knight** then opens on them in keep mode, to roll or pick which Knight they became. They keep their Virtues, GD, Age and everything they carry, and gain that Knight's Property, Ability, Passion and Seer. See [Choosing a Knight](Choosing-a-Knight#when-a-squire-is-knighted).
- Until that's done, their title bar shows **Choose Their Knight**.

A newly Knighted Squire gets a folder of their own in the Actors tab. If their portrait or Token was still the kneeling Squire picture, it is cleared.

## Dropping things onto the Knight sheet

| Drop this | From | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An item | Items tab, compendium, another sheet | A copy is added to the Knight. |
| A horse or pony | Actors tab, compendium | It becomes the steed the Knight rides. Replacing a living steed, or taking another Knight's, asks first. |
| Any other NPC | Actors tab, compendium | **Steed or Follower?** asks which. A follower rides with the Knight, shares their Remedies, can be taken up if the Knight falls, and stays when the Knight is re-chosen. A Warband is refused; muster it from a Domain. |
| A Squire | Actors tab | They become this Knight's Squire. |
| Another Knight | Actors tab | They are named this Knight's successor. |
| A Domain | Actors tab | The Knight rules it. |

Squires and Domains must come from the Actors tab, not a compendium. A steed or follower dropped from a compendium is brought into the world first. On a Squire's sheet, a dropped NPC is always their steed.

> **Did you know?**
> - Your steed and Squire are moved into your Knight's folder in the Actors tab as you take them.
> - Renaming the steed on its own sheet renames it on yours at once.
