# World Settings Reference

Every switch the system adds to Foundry's settings, in one place: its name as you see it, what it starts at, and what it changes. World settings belong to the Referee and apply to everyone in the world. Your own settings, further down, belong to each browser.

## Where to find them

As the Referee, open the **Game Settings** tab in the right-hand sidebar, press **Configure Settings**, and pick the **Mythic Bastionland** section. Most of these are also on the **Settings** tab of the [Referee Toolkit](The-Referee-Toolkit), where each world setting is marked **For the whole table.** Players see only their own settings. See [Your Settings and Accessibility](Your-Settings-and-Accessibility).

Only one setting needs a reload: **Campaign Timeline**. Foundry asks you to reload when you change it. Everything else applies straight away, or the next time the thing it affects is drawn.

## World settings

### Players and Knights

| Setting | Default | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Solo Play** | Off | For playing alone. The Realm keeps its Myths, Landmarks and Barriers from the Referee as it does from players, until the Company finds them. A Myth is named once one of its Omens is met. See [Solo Play](Solo-Play). |
| **Allow the Same Knight Twice** | Off | Lets more than one character be the same Knight from the book. Off, a Knight a living character already is can't be rolled, chosen or typed in again; a slain Knight's can. See [Choosing a Knight](Choosing-a-Knight). |
| **Let Players Open the Rulebook** | Off | Gives players the **Rulebook** button, the `B` key and the page links. Pages the Referee shows them open either way. See [The Rulebook Reader](The-Rulebook-Reader). |
| **Campaign Timeline** | On | The Timeline: a tab on each Knight, Squire and Domain sheet, a page in the Referee Toolkit and a window for everyone. Off, none of them show, though events are still written down for when it is turned on again. **Needs a reload.** See [The Campaign Timeline](The-Campaign-Timeline). |
| **Attack Effects on the Map** | On | Draws Attacks on the map and plays their sounds. Needs Sequencer and JB2A for the pictures, SoundFx Library for the sounds; without them, that half does nothing. See [Installing and Recommended Modules](Installing-and-Recommended-Modules). |

### The Realm

| Setting | Default | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Roll a New Hex's Land** | On | The first time the Company comes to rest in a Wilderness hex with nothing written or rolled there, its land and one feature are rolled on the Nature Spark Tables, kept in the hex, and whispered to the Referee. Holdings are left alone. See [Exploring the Wilderness](Exploring-the-Wilderness). |
| **Journal Entries for Hexes** | On | Each hex something is rolled, written or named for gets a Journal entry, kept up to date. Players can read its **What the Company Knows** page once they could open the hex in their Places; the **What's Here** page is the Referee's. See [Places and the Lay of the Land](Places-and-the-Lay-of-the-Land). |
| **Journal Entries for Sites** | On | Each Site with something drawn or written on it gets a Journal entry, hidden from players until you share it. See [Sites](Sites). |
| **Show Hex Column and Row** | On | Pointing at a hex of a Realm names its column and row, such as (5, 7), before what is there. Everyone at the table sees them. |
| **Dire Weather** | In Winter, as in most Realms | When the Realm suffers dire weather: **Never**, **In Winter, as in most Realms** or **All year round**. Dire Weather is then rolled as each Phase begins, unless the Company is in a Holding. See [Time, Phases and Seasons](Time-Phases-and-Seasons). |

### Weather on the map

All of these need FXMaster. Without it the weather is still rolled and kept, and none of it is drawn.

| Setting | Default | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Weather on the Map** | On | Draws the weather on the Scene the players are on. Off keeps the weather set but takes it off the map, the same as **Take It Off the Map** in the weather picker. |
| **Clouds** | On | The clouds drifting over the map, from a fair sky to a storm. |
| **Fog** | On | The fog over the map. |
| **Rain** | On | The rain, from a shower to a storm's. |
| **Hail** | On | The hail in a storm. |
| **Snow** | On | Falling snow. |
| **Blizzard Snow** | On | The driven snow of a blizzard. |
| **Storm Light** | On | The grey a storm casts over its clouds. Off, the clouds stay, untinted. |

On the Toolkit's Settings tab the seven kinds share one drop-down, **Types of Weather**.

## Your own settings

These are saved per browser, and each person sets their own. They sit in the same section of Configure Settings as the world settings.

| Setting | Default | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Text Size** | 1.00 (0.90 to 1.40) | The size of the writing, and the pictures beside it, on the system's sheets, windows and chat cards. |
| **Contrast** | Normal | **High Contrast** sharpens whichever colour scheme you read in. |
| **Colour Scheme** | Parchment | **Parchment**, **Lamplit (Dark, Warm)**, **Midnight (Dark, Blue)**, **Ashen (Dark, Charcoal)** or **Follow Foundry**. |
| **Typeface** | Old Print, as in the Book | Or **Plain Serif**, or **Signika, as in Foundry's Own Windows**. |
| **No Italic Text** | Off | Sets every slanted word upright, everywhere in Foundry. |
| **Reduce Motion** | Off | Turns off fades, pans the map straight to a hex, and drops the attack animations (the sounds stay). |
| **Rule Word Popups** | On | Hovering a rule word, such as Exposed or Hefty, shows what the book says it means. |
| **Larger Art on Hover** | On | A larger copy of a Knight's, Seer's or Myth's picture beside it while the pointer is over it. |
| **Reopen Sheets on Reload** | On | Reopens the sheets and the rulebook you had open when you reload Foundry. |
| **Phase, Season and Age at the Top of the Screen** | On | The time bar at the top of the screen. |
| **Travel Rules beside the Realm Map** | On | Travel on the left of a Realm's map, Rest and Exploration on its right. |
| **Name the Realm Hex under the Pointer** | On | The label under the map naming the hex you point at. |
| **Mark the Hexes the Company Has Been To** | On | The marks on every hex the Company has come into. |
| **Start the Tours by Themselves** | On | Whether the Realm Tours start on their own. Kept for every world you open in this browser. |
| **Reaching a New Hex** | Open the hex in Places | For the Referee: what happens when the Company comes to rest in a hex of the Realm you are looking at. The other choice is **Nothing**. |
| **Show the Weather Button** | On | For the Referee: the weather button in the Toolkit's banner, when FXMaster is on. Hidden, the weather last set stays and still follows the table. |

Their full descriptions are in [Your Settings and Accessibility](Your-Settings-and-Accessibility).

## Settings windows

Three buttons sit at the top of the system's section in Configure Settings.

| Button | Who | What it opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Choose the look** | Referee | **Realm Appearance**: the skin, colours and your own pictures a Realm Scene is drawn with. Each Realm Scene keeps its own look. See [Realm Appearance and Skins](Realm-Appearance-and-Skins). |
| **Choose the Mark** | Everyone | **Visited Marks**: how the hexes the Company has been to are marked in your browser, a pencilled ring, a tick, a cross or the hex's edge, each in a colour of your choosing. Right-clicking the footprints button beside the sidebar on a Realm opens it too. |
| **Open the Welcome** | Referee | **Welcome to Mythic Bastionland**, the window a new world opens with, where your rulebook PDF is brought in. See [Bringing In Your Rulebook](Bringing-In-Your-Rulebook). |
